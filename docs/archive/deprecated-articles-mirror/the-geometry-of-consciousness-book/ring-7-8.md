## From Trigrams to Hexagrams

Each hexagram is composed of two trigrams: a lower (inner) and an upper (outer). Since there are 8 trigrams, there are 8 × 8 = 64 possible hexagrams.

We can think of each hexagram as a *movement* within the cube:
- Where are you starting? (lower trigram)
- Where are you ending? (upper trigram)

The 64 hexagrams exhaust all possible start-end pairs on the 8-vertex cube.

## The Torus Knot

A torus is a donut shape — a surface of revolution with a hole in the middle. A torus knot is a closed curve that wraps around the torus in a specific pattern.

The notation (p,q) describes how many times the curve wraps around the torus:
- p = number of times around the hole (longitudinal)
- q = number of times through the hole (meridional)

A (2,1) torus knot wraps **twice** around the donut and **once** through the hole. It traces exactly the figure-8 pattern we described.

```
(2,1) TORUS KNOT:

Top view:               Side view:
    ╭──────╮               ╭──╮

  │ Monopole │       ───┼──────┼───

    ╰──╲╱──╯              ╰──╯
       ╳                    │
    ╭──╱╲──╮              ╭──╮

  │          │          │      │    ← ...and around again
  │          │       ───┼──────┼───
  │          │          │      │
   ╲        ╱            ╲    ╱
    ╰──────╯              ╰──╯

The Monopole (●) is the axis through the torus hole.
The path wraps twice around this axis without touching it.
```

The 64 hexagrams trace this torus knot path around the Monopole. The wheel we see (the Rave Mandala) is a *projection* of this three-dimensional structure onto a flat circle.

## Why 88° and Not 90°?

If the offset were exactly 90°, the two strands would be perfectly orthogonal — maximum separation, no interaction. This is **phase-lock**: two oscillators at exactly 90° can never sync because their peaks align with each other's zeros.

Phase-lock is stasis. It is the absence of dynamic tension. It is, in Ra's terminology, **death**.

The 2° deviation from 90° — making the offset 88° instead — creates **dynamic tension**. The strands are *almost* orthogonal but not quite. They are perpetually approaching phase-lock without ever achieving it.

This perpetual approach is **life**. It is the engine that drives change, the asymmetry that prevents stasis, the wobble that keeps the wheel turning.
