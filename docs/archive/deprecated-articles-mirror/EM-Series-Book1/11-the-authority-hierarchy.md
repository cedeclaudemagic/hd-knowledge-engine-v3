*Eleventh in a series exploring the electromagnetic structure of consciousness*

## Where We Are

We've built the complete framework for reading gates, lines, channels, circuits, and centres. We've seen how electromagnetic architecture determines function at every level.

Now we tackle one of the most contested aspects of Human Design: **Authority**.

Ra Uru Hu introduced the Authority hierarchy relatively late in his teaching. Many practitioners question why it takes the form it does. Why does emotional authority override everything else? Why do some authorities operate in the moment while others require time? Is the hierarchy arbitrary?

Here's what the electromagnetic framework reveals: **the Authority hierarchy follows wave mechanics**.

It's not arbitrary. It reflects the temporal characteristics of different positions on the wave. Once you see this, the hierarchy makes perfect sense.

---

## What Is Authority?

In Human Design, Authority is your reliable decision-making guidance — the inner signal you can trust. Unlike the mind (which can rationalise anything), Authority operates from the body's intelligence.

Different people have different Authorities based on their definition. The hierarchy determines which Authority takes precedence when multiple possibilities exist.

The standard hierarchy is:

1. Emotional (Solar Plexus)
2. Sacral
3. Splenic
4. Ego/Heart
5. Self-Projected (G Centre)
6. Mental/Environmental
7. Lunar (for Reflectors)

Why this order? Why does Emotional trump everything? Why is Sacral above Splenic?

The electromagnetic framework answers these questions.

## The Core Discovery

Authority follows wave mechanics because **different positions have different temporal characteristics**.

Some positions operate instantaneously — they give their signal in the moment, once, clearly.

Some positions operate over time — they require a cycle to complete before their signal is reliable.

The hierarchy reflects this temporal reality:

| Authority | Position | Temporal Mode | Why This Placement |
|-----------|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Emotional** | -2 (Voltage) | Wave over time | Voltage fluctuates — must wait for stability |
| **Sacral** | Mixed (includes -4, +1, +2) | Instantaneous | Source + response — fundamental life force |
| **Splenic** | -1 (Gate OUT) | Instantaneous | Discrimination threshold — speaks once, quietly |
| **Ego** | +1 (Gate IN) | Commitment-based | Initiation threshold — will entering form |
| **Self-Projected** | ±4 (Poles) | Resonance | Reference frame — identity through voice |
| **Mental** | Mixed | External | No inner authority — requires sounding board |
| **Lunar** | None | 28-day cycle | No definition — must sample entire field |

## Why Solar Plexus Has Absolute Priority

This is the key insight: **Voltage (-2) fluctuates and modulates ALL other signals.**

The Solar Plexus sits at position -2 — the voltage position. Voltage is pressure. Pressure that builds and releases. The emotional wave.

In electromagnetic terms, you cannot accurately read any other measurement while voltage is unstable. A voltmeter in a fluctuating field gives unreliable readings. You must wait for the wave to stabilise.

This is why Ra said "there is no truth in the now" for emotionally defined people.

Consider what happens in the emotional wave:

- At the **peak** (hope, excitement, enthusiasm), everything looks good. Every decision seems right. Every opportunity gleams.
- At the **trough** (disappointment, despair, flatness), nothing looks good. Every decision seems wrong. Every opportunity dims.
- At **neither peak nor trough** (the middle, the return to baseline), you can finally see clearly.

The signal from any other centre — Sacral response, Splenic intuition, Heart willpower — gets *modulated* by the emotional wave. The response that feels like a clear "yes" at the peak might feel like "no" at the trough. It's the same signal, distorted by voltage fluctuation.

**You cannot trust any instantaneous reading when voltage is unstable.**

This isn't psychological. It's electromagnetic. The wave is real. The distortion is real. Waiting for clarity isn't weakness — it's physics.

## The Instantaneous Authorities

Below Emotional authority, three authorities operate in the moment: Sacral, Splenic, and Ego. Each has distinct electromagnetic characteristics.

### Sacral Authority

The Sacral Centre includes gates at multiple positions, including:
- Position -4 (source pole)
- Positions +1 and +2 (gate in and current)

This spread gives the Sacral its character: **fundamental life force that responds**.

The Sacral doesn't deliberate. It responds. The "uh-huh" or "uhn-uhn" is current switching on or off — available or not available. This is position +2 (current) in action: flow that's either happening or not.

And because the Sacral includes source-pole energy (-4), its response connects to something fundamental. This isn't surface preference — it's life force itself declaring availability.

Sacral authority is instantaneous because current is instantaneous. Flow is happening or it isn't. Right now.

### Splenic Authority

The Spleen Centre has ALL gates at position -1 — the gate out. Pure discrimination.

Splenic authority is the quietest. It speaks once, in the moment, and doesn't repeat. This is the nature of gate-threshold awareness: the instant of discrimination, then it's gone.

Position -1 is where form meets potential. Where energy decides whether to cross. The Spleen knows *in that instant* whether something serves life or threatens it. But the instant passes. The signal doesn't linger.

Splenic authority is instantaneous because discrimination is instantaneous. The threshold is crossed or it isn't. The knowing happens now or not at all.

### Ego Authority

The Heart Centre has its anchor at position +1 — the gate in. The threshold of initiation. Where will becomes form.

Ego authority operates through commitment. "What's in it for me?" isn't selfishness — it's the gate-in asking whether this energy deserves to manifest. Whether the will is engaged.

Unlike Splenic (which discriminates once, quietly), Ego authority can repeat itself. Willpower can be reasserted. The commitment can be renewed. This is because +1 is the gate *into* form — energy entering and re-entering manifestation.

Ego authority is commitment-based: not a single flash but an ongoing willingness to engage.

---

## The Reference Authority

### Self-Projected Authority

The G Centre holds both poles — position -4 (source) and position +4 (sink). The complete reference frame.

Self-Projected authority operates through resonance. You hear your truth by speaking it. The voice carries identity, and when you hear yourself say something, you know whether it resonates with your reference frame.

This isn't about what you say being true for others. It's about hearing whether what you say aligns with your own polar structure. Does this statement resonate with my source (-4)? Does it ground properly in my form (+4)?

Self-Projected authority requires speaking — externalising the signal so it can be heard and compared against the internal reference. It's not instantaneous like Sacral or Splenic, but it's not wave-dependent like Emotional either. It operates through resonance: does this vibrate true?

## The Authorities Without Inner Signal

### Mental/Environmental Authority

When none of the above centres are defined, there's no reliable inner authority. The mind can rationalise anything. Without definition in Solar Plexus, Sacral, Spleen, Heart, or G Centre, where does guidance come from?

**From the environment. From sounding boards. From others.**

Mental authority isn't really authority at all — it's the recognition that this person needs external reflection to make decisions. Talk it through. Find the right environment. Notice what places and people bring clarity.

The electromagnetic reading: no defined position to generate reliable signal. The wave positions that create Emotional, Sacral, Splenic, Ego, and Self-Projected authority simply aren't defined. Guidance must come from the field, not from internal definition.

### Lunar Authority

Reflectors have no definition at all. Nothing fixed. Nothing consistent. The entire bodygraph is open.

How does someone with no fixed positions make decisions?

**By sampling the entire field over time.**

Specifically, over one lunar cycle — approximately 28 days. As the Moon moves through all 64 gates, the Reflector samples each position. By the end of the cycle, they've felt every part of the wave. They know how they felt about the decision at every position.

This isn't waiting for emotional clarity (which requires a defined Solar Plexus). It's waiting to sample the *entire electromagnetic field* through the Moon's transit.

Lunar authority is time-based, but differently than Emotional. Emotional waits for *their* wave to stabilise. Lunar waits to feel the decision from *every* position, using the Moon as the hand that moves them around the wheel.

## Why This Hierarchy

The hierarchy makes electromagnetic sense:

**Emotional first** because voltage fluctuation distorts all other signals. You cannot trust an instantaneous reading while the wave is moving. Wait.

**Sacral second** (when no Emotional definition) because life force response is the most fundamental signal. Source-pole energy declaring availability.

**Splenic third** because gate-threshold discrimination is instantaneous but quieter than Sacral. It speaks once. Miss it and it's gone.

**Ego fourth** because gate-in initiation operates through commitment, not single response. Willpower can engage and re-engage.

**Self-Projected fifth** because polar reference operates through resonance, requiring externalisation. Speak to hear.

**Mental/Lunar last** because without definition, there's no internal signal to trust. Guidance must come from outside — from environment or from time.

This isn't arbitrary. It's wave mechanics. Temporal characteristics determine the hierarchy.

## The Practical Implication

Understanding Authority electromagnetically changes how you use it.

**If you have Emotional Authority:** You're not being indecisive. You're waiting for voltage to stabilise. The wave is real. The distortion at peak and trough is real. Find your baseline.

**If you have Sacral Authority:** Trust the instant response. Don't let the mind override current. The "uh-huh" or "uhn-uhn" is life force speaking. It knows.

**If you have Splenic Authority:** Listen for the quiet, once. It won't repeat. The discrimination happens at the threshold and then it's gone. Trust the first knowing.

**If you have Ego Authority:** Check your commitment. Is the will engaged? What's in it for you? Not selfish — essential. The gate into form only opens for what deserves to manifest.

**If you have Self-Projected Authority:** Speak to hear yourself. The resonance with your reference frame tells you what's true for you. Find listeners who let you hear yourself.

**If you have Mental/Environmental Authority:** Stop trying to find inner authority. Find the right places, the right people, the sounding boards that bring clarity. Trust the environment.

**If you have Lunar Authority:** Take the full cycle. Feel the decision from every position as the Moon transits. By day 28, you'll know what you couldn't know on day 1.

## Authority as Wave Mechanics

The Authority hierarchy isn't a rule imposed from outside. It's a description of how different electromagnetic positions operate in time.

Some positions give instantaneous signals. Some require time to stabilise. Some provide reference frames. Some aren't defined at all.

Knowing your Authority means knowing which position(s) you can trust, and how that position operates temporally. The framework doesn't change your Authority — it explains why your Authority works the way it does.

And in that understanding, using it becomes simpler. You're not following arbitrary rules. You're working with wave mechanics.
