## Success Criteria

### PROVEN (Move to /docs/framework/)

- [ ] Geometric mechanism explicit

- [ ] Connects to proven framework
- [ ] All exceptions explained
- [ ] Multiple paths converge

### MAPPED (Stay in /docs/investigations/)

- [ ] Pattern identified
- [ ] Mechanism proposed but not proven necessary
- [ ] Some exceptions unexplained

### EMPIRICAL (Move to /docs/investigations/boundaries/)

- [ ] Confirmed as transmitted content
- [ ] Geometry constrains but doesn't determine
- [ ] Document what WAS derivable vs what wasn't

### OUTSTANDING (Update status file)

- [ ] Inconclusive
- [ ] More investigation needed
- [ ] Document what was tried and why it didn't resolve
