## The Derivation Gradient

Structure → Character → Vocabulary

1. **Structure**: Geometrically necessary (PROVEN)
   - Gate 1 is a Standing Wave gate (inner = outer = Heaven)
   - Gate 1 occupies position -4 (Source)

2. **Character**: Derivable from structure (MAPPED)
   - Gate 1 is pure void, maximum yang
   - Gate 1 is the source pole of the G Centre

3. **Vocabulary**: Received from transmission (EMPIRICAL)
   - Gate 1 is called "The Creative"
   - Gate 1 is about self-expression and creativity

---

## Key Finding

**Structure generates character; vocabulary is received.**

The geometry tells us WHAT a gate does functionally. The transmission tells us what to CALL it and how to INTERPRET it for humans.
