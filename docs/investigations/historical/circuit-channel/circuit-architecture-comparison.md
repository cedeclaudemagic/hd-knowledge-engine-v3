## Transformation Density

How much of each circuit crosses zero:

| Circuit | Cross-Zero | Same-Phase | Doubled | Transformation % |
|---------|------------|------------|---------|------------------|
| Knowing | 5 of 18 | 10 | 3 | **28%** |
| Understanding | 6 of 14 | 6 | 2 | 43% |
| Integration | 2 of 4 | 1 | 1 | 50% |
| Sensing | 11 of 14 | 1 | 2 | **79%** |

**Pattern:** Transformation density inversely correlates with anchoring. Maximum anchoring (Knowing) = minimum transformation. Flow anchoring (Sensing) = maximum transformation.

**Important distinction:** Low transformation ≠ low change capacity. The framework contains two change mechanisms:
- **Transformation** (zero-crossing) — continuous, gradual change
- **Mutation** (phase alignment) — sudden, pulse-like change

Knowing's 28% transformation enables mutation through phase-lock between coupled circulators. The most mutative circuit transforms least because **mutation requires stability, not flux**. See [Knowing Circuit Analysis](knowing-circuit-analysis.md#mutation-vs-transformation) for full theory.

## Predictive Framework

Given the pattern, we can predict architectural features for unanalyzed circuits:

### If a circuit needs rapid switching:
- Expect single anchor at gate position
- Expect commutator-type channel structure
- Expect moderate transformation density

### If a circuit needs stable reference:
- Expect dual anchors at storage positions
- Expect all transformers to be regulated
- Expect balanced transformation/circulation

### If a circuit needs continuous flow:
- Expect dual anchors at flow positions
- Expect oscillator channels
- Expect high transformation density
- Expect release pathways (parallel dematerialisers)

### If a circuit needs stable recognition:
- Expect maximum anchoring (multiple axes)
- Expect double-circulator channels
- Expect very low transformation density
- Expect cross-domain circulation without zero-crossing

## The Inverse Relationship

A structural finding emerges from comparing all four circuits:

```
Anchors:        3          2          1          2
                │          │          │          │
Trans %:       28%        43%        50%        79%
                │          │          │          │
Circuit:    Knowing   Understanding  Integration  Sensing
```

**More anchoring correlates with less transformation.**

This makes electromagnetic sense:
- **Knowing** has maximum reference (poles + gate) — stable enough to recognize without transforming
- **Sensing** has flow reference only — must transform continuously to process experience

The spectrum runs from maximum stability (Knowing) to maximum flux (Sensing), with the transition circuits in between.
