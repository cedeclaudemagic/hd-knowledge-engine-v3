## The Three Planetary Classes

| Earth | 0.74 | Grounding - material anchor |

| Planet | Ratio | Primary Function |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| Mercury | 0.33 | Translating - mental processing |
| Mars | 0.31 | Initiating - explosive force |

**Commonality**: These planets carry energies that the system generally cannot absorb. They need very specific positions to function.

**The Mars Pattern**: Initiation serves at entry points, generators, and material gates. It corrupts at development positions, expression (Throat), and emotional processing (Solar Plexus).

## Electromagnetic Principles from Planet Profiles

### Principle 1: Exalters vs Shadow Planets
The system has "positive" planets (Sun, Saturn) that serve most positions and "shadow" planets (Mars, Mercury) that need specific contexts.

### Principle 2: Line Position Affinity
- **Line 1**: Venus (foundation) vs Mercury shadow
- **Line 2**: Sun (development) vs Moon shadow
- **Line 3**: Saturn (inner completion)
- **Line 4**: Moon, Pluto (mode-shift) vs Sun shadow
- **Line 5**: Mars (outer development)
- **Line 6**: Sun, Saturn (completion) vs Pluto shadow

### Principle 3: Centre Affinity
- **G Centre**: Venus, Mercury rare home
- **Throat**: Sun home, Mercury shadow
- **Sacral**: Mars home, Pluto shadow
- **Root**: Saturn home, Venus shadow
- **Spleen**: Pluto home
- **Solar Plexus**: Mars shadow

### Principle 4: Circuit Affinity
- **Knowing**: Saturn dominant (6.5:1)
- **Integration**: Moon shadow (0/8 detriments confirmed)
- **Sensing**: Mars, Venus both have complex relationships
- **Ego**: Saturn shadow

### Principle 5: Gate Type Affinity
- **Same-phase-void**: Pluto dominant (3.75:1)
- **Cross-zero**: Sun strong (8.5:1 dematerialising)
- **Doubled**: Venus home (2.25:1)
