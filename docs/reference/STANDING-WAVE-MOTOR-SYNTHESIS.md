## The Two-Tier Hierarchy

### Mathematical Classification (Position -1)

Standing waves split into two tiers based on **palindrome structure**:

| Tier | Gates | Binary Property | Phase-Invariance |
|------|-------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Absolute Anchors** | 1, 2, 29, 30 | Palindromic | 100% (invariant across all 384 phase shifts) |
| **Secondary Anchors** | 51, 52, 57, 58 | Non-palindromic | 67-83% (stable in most phase shifts) |

**The proof**: A 6-bit pattern is palindromic if bit positions 1-6 mirror as 6-1, 5-2, 4-3. For doubled trigrams, this requires the trigram itself to be palindromic (bit 1 = bit 3).

Four trigrams are palindromic: Heaven (111), Earth (000), Fire (101), Water (010)
Four are not: Thunder (100), Mountain (001), Wind (011), Lake (110)

**Source**: Position -1, Chapter 2 (The Palindrome Principle)

### Operational Correlation (Position +3)

| Mathematical Property | Operational Consequence |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Palindromic (100% phase-invariant) | Occupies flow positions (±2) or poles (±4) |
| Non-palindromic (67-83% phase-stable) | Occupies threshold (±1) or storage (±3) positions |

## The Motor Principle: Mathematical → Operational

### Why Motors Need Standing Waves (Position +3, Chapter 6)

To generate energy, a centre needs **sustained oscillation**. A one-time pulse is discharge, not generation. Sustained oscillation requires an anchor point—something that holds position while the energy oscillates around it.

Standing waves provide this anchor. They oscillate without traveling. The energy moves, but the pattern holds.

### Why Standing Waves Are Reliable (Position -1, Chapter 5)

Standing waves are structurally privileged because:

1. **Phase-invariance**: They persist across phase shifts that would transform other gates
2. **Periodicity**: They're Period-1 or Period-3 patterns (maximum regularity)
3. **Self-complementarity**: Each standing wave's complement is also a standing wave

### The Bridge: Math IS Mechanism

| Motor | Standing Wave Gate | EM Position | Palindrome? | Phase-Invariance | Authority Behaviour |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|-------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Sacral** | 29 (Water/Water) | +2 (Current) | Yes | 100% | Instant response |
| **Solar Plexus** | 30 (Fire/Fire) | -2 (Voltage) | Yes | 100% | Wave requires waiting |
| **Heart** | 51 (Thunder/Thunder) | +1 (Gate In) | No | 67% | Renewable commitment |
| **Root** | 52, 58 | ±3 (Storage) | No | 83%, 83% | Pressure pulses |

**Key insight**: The Sacral (Gate 29) and Solar Plexus (Gate 30) are BOTH 100% phase-invariant, but their EM positions differ:
- Sacral at +2 (current/flow) → signal is steady, response is instant
- Solar Plexus at -2 (voltage/amplitude) → signal oscillates, requires wave completion

**Same mathematical privilege, different positional behaviour.** The math doesn't change, but where you sit on the wave determines how the stability manifests.
