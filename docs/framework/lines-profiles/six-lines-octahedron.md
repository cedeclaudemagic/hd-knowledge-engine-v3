## Core Finding

The 6 Lines of a hexagram map to the 6 vertices of an octahedron — three perpendicular axes with two directions each. This is not metaphor but geometric structure: 6 = 3 × 2.

---

## The Hexagram Structure

A hexagram is six lines stacked vertically:

```
Line 6  ━━━━━━━━━  ┐
Line 5  ━━━  ━━━   │ Outer Trigram (destination)
Line 4  ━━━━━━━━━  ┘
        ─────────── threshold (3→4 transition)
Line 3  ━━━  ━━━   ┐
Line 2  ━━━━━━━━━  │ Inner Trigram (origin)
Line 1  ━━━  ━━━   ┘
```

- **Lines 1, 2, 3:** Inner trigram (origin)
- **Lines 4, 5, 6:** Outer trigram (destination)
- **Threshold 3→4:** The crossing between trigrams

## The Octahedron (6 Vertices)

An octahedron has 6 vertices — the 6 face normals of a cube:

```
THE OCTAHEDRON (6 Vertices = 6 Lines)

                Line 6 (+Z)
                    ●
                    │
                    │
    Line 3 (+Y) ●───┼───● Line 4 (-Y)
               ╱    │    ╲
              ╱     │     ╲
    Line 1 ●───────●───────● Line 2
    (+X)            │        (-X)
                    │
                    ●
                Line 5 (-Z)
```

The 6 lines map to 3 axes with 2 directions each:

| Axis | Positive (+) | Negative (-) |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| X-axis | Line 1 | Line 2 |
| Y-axis | Line 3 | Line 4 |
| Z-axis | Line 6 | Line 5 |

| Pair | Axis | Relationship |

| Line 1 ↔ Line 4 | X ↔ -Y | Harmonic resonance |
| Line 2 ↔ Line 5 | -X ↔ -Z | Harmonic resonance |

## The 3→4 Threshold

The transition from Line 3 to Line 4 is geometrically significant:

- **Line 3:** Top of inner trigram, completion of origin
- **Line 4:** Bottom of outer trigram, entry to destination

This threshold marks the **flip from inward (-) to outward (+) orientation** — the crossing between domains.

```
        LINE 4 (-Y)
            │
            │
    LINE 3 ─┼─ LINE 4
    (+Y)    │   (-Y)
            │
            ▼
      THRESHOLD
```

## Cube-Octahedron Duality

The octahedron is the **dual** of the cube:
- 8 cube vertices ↔ 8 octahedron faces
- 6 cube faces ↔ 6 octahedron vertices
- 12 edges ↔ 12 edges

The 6 Lines are the 6 face normals of the trigram cube — directions perpendicular to each cube face.

| Octahedron Vertex | Direction | Cube Face | Line |
|-------------------|-----------|-----------|------|
| 1 | +X | Right | Line 1 |
| 2 | -X | Left | Line 2 |
| 3 | +Y | Front | Line 3 |
| 4 | -Y | Back | Line 4 |
| 5 | -Z | Bottom | Line 5 |
| 6 | +Z | Top | Line 6 |

## The Lower and Upper Trigram Split

| Lines | Trigram | Octahedron Position |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| 1, 2, 3 | Inner (origin) | +X, -X, +Y vertices |
| 4, 5, 6 | Outer (destination) | -Y, -Z, +Z vertices |

The hexagram journey moves from one hemisphere of the octahedron to the other.

## Why 6?

The number 6 is not arbitrary:

- **6 = 3 × 2:** Three spatial dimensions, two polarities
- **6 = octahedron vertices:** Minimum directions to span 3D space
- **6 = cube faces:** The "where to look" from the cube centre

Any system encoding 3D spatial information needs exactly 6 fundamental directions.
