## Why 88°?

The wheel has 360°. The offset is **88°**, which equals:

**88° / 360° × 8 steps = 1.956 steps**

Not exactly 2 steps (90°). Not exactly 1 step. **Almost perpendicular, but not quite.**

| Offset | Steps | Result |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 90° | 2.00 | Perfect perpendicularity → **death** |
| 88° | 1.96 | 0.04 step deviation → **life** |
