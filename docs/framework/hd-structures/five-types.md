## Overview

Human Design Types emerge from the structural configuration of defined centres and their connections. Type is determined by two factors:

1. **Sacral Definition** - Whether the Sacral Centre is defined
2. **Motor-to-Throat Connection** - Whether any motor centre connects to the Throat

This creates four Types with distinct electromagnetic signatures.

## Part 4: Electromagnetic Signatures by Type

### Manifestor Signature

**Configuration:** Motor-to-Throat WITHOUT Sacral

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Direct transformation pathway to expression
- No sustained generation capacity (no Sacral source)
- "Closed and repelling aura" = electromagnetic boundary that deflects rather than envelops
- Action initiates from voltage/pressure (SP), will (Heart), or stored energy (Root)

**Key channels:**
- 12-22: Emotional expression (SP → Throat)
- 21-45: Willful expression (Heart → Throat)
- 35-36: Experiential expression (SP → Throat)

### Generator Signature

**Configuration:** Defined Sacral

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Sacral gates cluster at source (-4) and gate/flow (+1/+2)
- Source connection = sustained generation capacity
- "Open and enveloping aura" = electromagnetic field that draws in and surrounds
- Response mechanism uses the +1/+2 gates (Thunder/Water) for in-the-moment engagement

**Pure Generator:** Sacral defined, no motor-to-Throat
- Energy must be requested/invited to reach expression
- Transformation happens internally first

**Manifesting Generator:** Sacral + motor-to-Throat
- Same generative capacity as Generator
- PLUS direct pathway to expression
- Can initiate AND sustain

### Projector Signature

**Configuration:** No Sacral, no motor-to-Throat

**Electromagnetic character:**
- "Focused and absorbing aura" = directed electromagnetic field
- No internal generation capacity
- No direct expression pathway
- Function: Guide, direct, manage others' energy

**Gate concentration:**
- Awareness centres (Spleen, Ajna, Solar Plexus)
- Identity centre (G)
- Non-motor pathways

### Reflector Signature

**Configuration:** No defined centres

**Electromagnetic character:**
- "Resistant and sampling aura" = reflective electromagnetic field
- No consistent internal signature
- Reflects the conditioning field
- Lunar authority (28-day cycle) = sampling all configurations

## Summary

### Type Creation Rules

1. **Reflector:** No defined centres
2. **Projector:** At least one centre defined, but no Sacral AND no motor-to-Throat
3. **Manifestor:** Motor connected to Throat WITHOUT Sacral definition
4. **Generator:** Sacral defined WITHOUT motor-to-Throat
5. **Manifesting Generator:** Sacral defined WITH motor-to-Throat

### Critical Channels

| Channel | Creates | Mechanism |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| 12-22 | Manifestor (or MG) | SP → Throat |
| 21-45 | Manifestor (or MG) | Heart → Throat |
| 35-36 | Manifestor (or MG) | SP → Throat |
| 20-34 | MG | Sacral → Throat |
| Any Sacral channel | Generator | Defines Sacral |

### Electromagnetic Patterns

- **Manifestor channels** are all double-transformers (high energy exchange)
- **Generator channels** include standing-wave anchors (sustained capacity)
- **Projector channels** are mostly projected (require invitation/recognition)
- The **MG channel** (20-34) uniquely combines both signatures
