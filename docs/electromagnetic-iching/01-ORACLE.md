# The Electromagnetic I Ching

*384 Lines. 384 Principles. The Wave Revealed.*

---

## Introduction

The I Ching is electromagnetic.

For three thousand years, this has been encoded in imagery — dragons rising, walls falling, wine spilling, thunder shocking. The images resonate because they touch real structure. But the structure itself remained hidden.

This oracle reveals the mechanics.

Trigrams are positions on a wave, numbered -4 to +4. Hexagrams are vectors — movements from one position to another. Lines are states within those positions: entry, development, completion — first internally, then externally.

What follows is not interpretation. It is the wave speaking as itself.

## The Line Structure

Every hexagram has six lines. The first three operate within the inner trigram; the second three within the outer trigram.

| Line | Position | Function |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Entry-Inner | First contact with inner trigram |
| 2 | Development-Inner | The inner trigram's clearest expression |
| 3 | Completion-Inner | Peak of inner trigram, pre-threshold |
| 4 | Entry-Outer | First contact with outer trigram |
| 5 | Development-Outer | The outer trigram's clearest expression |
| 6 | Completion-Outer | Peak of outer trigram, gate completion |

**Line 1** is the foundation. What's established here determines everything that follows:
- **Conditions:** Entry sets the terms. Wrong foundation destabilises the entire structure.
- **Timing:** Line 1 is where timing matters most — the right frequency at the right moment.
- **Introspection:** Not yet visible, turned inward. The foundation is laid before anything can be seen.

**Lines 2 and 5** anchor their respective trigrams — they ARE the position, not entering or leaving it. These are the standing wave points within each trigram, the clearest expression of the frequency.

**Line 6** is completion at the edge. Three qualities converge here:
- **Perspective:** From Line 6, the entire circuit is visible. Every position has been traversed. The whole pattern is comprehensible.
- **Timing:** Line 6 doesn't act until it sees the complete picture. Action from partial position is premature action.
- **Mutation:** Line 6 is the membrane between this gate and the next in sequence. What completes here seeds what begins there. The wave doesn't end — it transforms.

The transition between Line 3 and Line 4 is always significant:
- In **standing waves**: an expression-shift (internal → external, same position)
- In **cross-zero gates**: domain crossing (void ↔ form)
- In **same-phase gates**: position shift within domain

---

## Amplitude

Amplitude is the distance between inner and outer trigram positions.

| Amplitude | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| 0 | Standing wave — holds position |
| 2 | Minimum crossing (Gates axis) |
| 4 | Flow axis crossing |
| 6 | Mixed axis crossing |
| 8 | Maximum crossing (Poles axis) |

Higher amplitude means greater transformation, longer timing, more positions traversed.
