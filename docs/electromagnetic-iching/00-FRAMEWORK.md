## Core Premise

The I Ching is an electromagnetic system. Trigrams are positions on a wave (-4 to +4). Hexagrams are vectors — inner position to outer position. Lines are states within those positions. The structure *is* the meaning.

This oracle doesn't interpret imagery. It reveals mechanics.

| Gate | Position | Frequency |

| 51 | +1 | Gate-IN |

| 58 | -3 | Capacitance |

**Structure:** 
- Lines 1-3: Internal experience of position
- Lines 4-6: External expression of position
- Transition 3→4: Expression-shift (same position, different orientation)

**Completion character varies by position:**
- Poles (-4, +4): Noble/receptive — stable when held
- Storage (-3, +3): Accumulative — builds when held
- Flow (-2, +2): Problematic — must move, held = trapped
- Gates (-1, +1): Limit — don't push further

## Amplitude

**Amplitude = |Outer Position - Inner Position|**

| Amplitude | Character | Example |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| 0 | Standing wave — holds position | Gate 1 (-4 → -4) |
| 2 | Minimum crossing — Gates axis | Gate 32 (-1 → +1) |
| 4 | Flow axis crossing | Gate 63 (-2 → +2) |
| 6 | Mixed axis crossing | Gate 5 (-4 → +2) |
| 8 | Maximum — Poles axis | Gate 11 (-4 → +4) |

**Amplitude determines:**
- Intensity of transformation
- Time/energy required for crossing
- Scope of what's traversed

Higher amplitude = more positions crossed = greater transformation = longer timing.

## The Four Axes

### Poles Axis (Heaven ↔ Earth, -4 ↔ +4)
- Maximum amplitude when crossed
- Source and Sink — origin and completion
- Pure potential ↔ pure form
- "Great" transformations

### Flow Axis (Fire ↔ Water, -2 ↔ +2)
- Voltage and Current
- Pressure ↔ movement
- The carrier wave — how energy actually moves
- Completion and new beginning (Gates 63/64)

### Gates Axis (Wind ↔ Thunder, -1 ↔ +1)
- Threshold positions — closest to zero
- Release ↔ arousal
- Where void and form touch
- Minimum amplitude crossing

### Storage Axis (Lake ↔ Mountain, -3 ↔ +3)
- Capacitance and Inductance
- Stored potential ↔ stored form
- Accumulation in both domains
- Joy ↔ stillness

Planets condition each line — exalted supports, detriment undermines.

**Note:** Planetary assignments are empirical, not derived. We include them because they operate, not because we can derive them from first principles. 5. **Domain is quality** — void (potential) vs form (manifest)
6. **Practical without being prescriptive** — explain what's happening, let reader apply
7. **Planetary conditioning included honestly** — it operates, we include it, we don't claim derivation
