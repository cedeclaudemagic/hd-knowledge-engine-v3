## Key Observations

### 1. Standing Wave Distribution Across Pillars

Each Pillar contains exactly **2 standing wave gates**:

| Pillar | Standing Waves | Positions |
|--------|----------------|-----------|
| Fire (1) | 1, 30 | -4, -2 |
| Water (2) | 2, 29 | +4, +2 |
| Truth (63) | 52, 57 | +3, -1 |
| Light (64) | 51, 58 | +1, -3 |

**Pattern**: The standing waves are distributed such that:
- Fire contains the **two void-side flow positions** (-4, -2)
- Water contains the **two material-side flow positions** (+4, +2)
- Truth contains **storage** (+3) and **gate-out** (-1)
- Light contains **gate-in** (+1) and **capacitance** (-3)

This is a remarkable correspondence: the Pillar elements map to electromagnetic functions!

### 2. The Cross-Zero Pillars (63, 64)

Gates 63 and 64 are both Pillars AND cross-zero gates. This is structurally unique:
- **63 (Water/Fire)**: -2 inner → +2 outer = manifesting
- **64 (Fire/Water)**: +2 inner → -2 outer = dematerialising

They are **exact inverses** of each other, just as Gates 1 and 2 are standing waves at opposite poles.

### 3. Mystery-Level Patterns

The 12 Mysteries show interesting EM type distributions:

**Fire's Mysteries (28, 43, 44)**: Mixed types
**Water's Mysteries (23, 24, 27)**: All same-side material
**Truth's Mysteries (38, 40, 54)**: All cross-zero
**Light's Mysteries (37, 39, 53)**: Mixed types

**Hypothesis**: Mysteries under cross-zero Pillars (63, 64) may have more cross-zero children.
