# Phase 5: Line Position Synthesis

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines planetary distribution across Lines 1-6 to understand which planets favor which positions. The data reveals a fundamental electromagnetic logic: inner lines (1-3) have different planetary affinities than outer lines (4-6), corresponding to the inner/outer trigram structure.

## Dominant Planets by Line

### Line 1 - Entry Inner (Foundation)

**Dominant Exalt: Venus (12) / Sun (12) tied**
**Dominant Detri: Mars (17)**

Venus and Sun both excel at foundational entry. Venus brings harmony to the beginning; Sun brings illumination to introspection.

Mars corrupts foundations - force destroys the delicate work of establishing beginnings.

### Line 2 - Development Inner (Projection)

**Dominant Exalt: Sun (13)**
**Dominant Detri: Mars (19)**

Sun leads development - illumination serves the process of inner growth and projection.

Mars at its worst here (19) - forcing development destroys natural unfolding.

### Line 3 - Completion Inner (Adaptation)

**Dominant Exalt: Saturn (9)**
**Dominant Detri: Mars (16)**

Saturn excels at inner completion - structure and discipline bring the inner process to its proper end.

Mars still corrupts, but less than at L2 - some force may be needed for adaptation.

### Line 4 - Mode Shift / Entry Outer (Opportunity)

**Dominant Exalt: Jupiter (12)**
**Dominant Detri: Mars (19)**

Jupiter leads the shift from inner to outer - expansion serves externalization and opportunity.

Mars equals its L2 high (19) - forcing the mode shift destroys the natural transition.

### Line 5 - Development Outer (Universalization)

**Dominant Exalt: Jupiter (10)**
**Dominant Detri: Mars (15)**

Jupiter continues to dominate - expansion serves universalization, reaching outward.

Mars drops slightly - outer development can tolerate more force than inner.

### Line 6 - Completion Outer (Transition)

**Dominant Exalt: Sun (11)**
**Dominant Detri: Pluto (11)**

Sun returns to dominance - illumination serves the final transition.

Pluto replaces Mars as top detriment - at completion, transformation itself becomes the threat. The position is already about death/rebirth; excessive transformation corrupts the proper ending.

## Inner vs Outer Trigram Patterns

### Inner Trigram Lines (1-3)

**Best Exaltations:**
- L1: Venus (12), Sun (12), Neptune (9)
- L2: Sun (13)
- L3: Saturn (9)

**Pattern:** The inner trigram favours illumination (Sun), harmony (Venus), and structure (Saturn). Inner work requires light, balance, and discipline.

**Worst Detriments:**
- L1: Mars (17), Mercury (11), Venus (11)
- L2: Mars (19), Moon (12)
- L3: Mars (16), Jupiter (10)

**Pattern:** Force (Mars) destroys inner work. Communication (Mercury) disrupts foundation. Fluctuation (Moon) disrupts development.

### Outer Trigram Lines (4-6)

**Best Exaltations:**
- L4: Jupiter (12), Moon (11), Pluto (10)
- L5: Jupiter (10), Uranus (6)
- L6: Sun (11), diverse others

**Pattern:** The outer trigram favours expansion (Jupiter), transformation (Pluto), and disruption (Uranus). External work requires growth, change, and innovation.

**Worst Detriments:**
- L4: Mars (19)
- L5: Mars (15), Jupiter (8)
- L6: Pluto (11), Jupiter (9)

**Pattern:** Force still corrupts (Mars) but decreases toward completion. Transformation (Pluto) threatens final transition.

## Summary: The Line Position Principles

### Principle 1: Mars Corrupts All But Completion
Mars is the dominant detriment at Lines 1-5 but drops to 3rd at Line 6. Force destroys all stages except final transition.

### Principle 2: Inner Lines Favour Clarity
Sun, Venus, and Saturn dominate inner line (1-3) exaltations. Inner work requires illumination, harmony, and structure.

### Principle 3: Outer Lines Favour Expansion
Jupiter dominates outer line (4-5) exaltations. External work requires growth and expansion.

### Principle 4: Line 6 Is Unique
At completion, the pattern shifts:
- Sun returns as dominant exalt (illumination for transition)
- Pluto becomes dominant detri (transformation threatens completion)
- Mars drops to 3rd place

### Principle 5: Line 4 Is the Pivot
The mode shift position (Line 4) has the most anomalies (3) and the most diverse exaltations. The transition from inner to outer is the most variable position.

### Principle 6: Some Positions Are Incorruptible
6 positions have no detriment - family foundation, survival intuition, innocence, sainthood, generosity, and final surrender cannot be corrupted.
