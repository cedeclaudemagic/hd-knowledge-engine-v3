### The Primary Opposition: Moon/Mars

With 28 total mutual oppositions, Moon and Mars represent the fundamental polarity between **receptive awareness** and **active force**.

- When Moon exalts, Mars corrupts (22 times) - receptivity threatened by force
- When Mars exalts, Moon corrupts (6 times) - action undermined by fluctuation

This 22:6 ratio shows that Moon more often "wins" - suggesting the system favours awareness over action.

The Moon/Mars dynamic (28 mutual oppositions) shows that receptive awareness more often succeeds where force would fail.

Moon/Mars, Venus/Pluto, Sun/Earth - these reciprocal oppositions reveal the basic polarities of the system.

Rare pairs concentrate at standing wave gates, where the doubled trigram creates unique electromagnetic conditions.

The same pair (Venus/Moon) means different things in different circuits - self-coherence in Integration, emotional stability in Sensing.
