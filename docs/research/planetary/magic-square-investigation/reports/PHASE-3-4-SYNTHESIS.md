## Part 5: Predictive Model Results

### The Failure of Vertical Priors

| Model | Accuracy |
|-------|----------|
| Baseline (most frequent at pos,line) | 39.1% |
| Vertical-aware (weighted by E/D) | 37.0% |
| Improvement | **-2.1%** |

**Why didn't it work?**

The vertical E/D bias is a POPULATION-LEVEL phenomenon. It tells us:
- "Beta planets will appear more often as detriments overall"
- It does NOT tell us "this specific line will have a Beta detriment"

The baseline model already captures the most frequent planet at each (position, line) combination. Adding vertical priors doesn't help because:
1. The baseline already reflects the vertical bias
2. Individual lines don't consistently follow the population pattern
