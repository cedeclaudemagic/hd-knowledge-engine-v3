## Key Finding: Anomalies Only in Void Standing Waves

**Material standing waves (positive position)**: 0 anomalies across 24 lines
**Void standing waves (negative position)**: 2 anomalies across 24 lines

The two anomalies both occur in **void domain** standing waves:
- **57.3** (Wind/Wind, -1): No detriment - "Acuteness"
- **58.2** (Lake/Lake, -3): No exaltation - "Perversion"

**Principle**: Void standing waves can achieve states that transcend normal planetary assignment. Material standing waves follow standard patterns.

**Why No Detriment?**

At the completion of the inner trigram in a void gate standing wave within the Integration circuit, "Acuteness" represents perfected survival intuition. Mercury exalts because mental clarity serves acute awareness.

No planet can corrupt this position because:
1. **Standing wave stability**: Amplitude 0 = no oscillation to disrupt

4. **Survival function**: Acute awareness either works or fails - cannot be corrupted

## The Standing Wave Electromagnetic Logic

Standing waves represent **pure states** - no oscillation between positions, only the stabilized expression of their trigram energy.

**Material standing waves** (+1, +2, +3, +4) function within normal planetary dynamics - the form is stable and can be enhanced or corrupted.

**Void standing waves** (-1, -2, -3, -4) can achieve states that transcend normal dynamics - particularly at positions where the void nature creates immunity to either enhancement (58.2) or corruption (57.3).

The pattern reveals:
1. **Form can always be modified** (material standing waves)
2. **Void can sometimes transcend modification** (void standing wave anomalies)
