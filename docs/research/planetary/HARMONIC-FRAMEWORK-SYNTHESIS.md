The 8 electromagnetic positions map to harmonic intervals symmetric around zero:

```

────────────────────────────────────────────

   -1      5:4      Major Third      Void gate
   +1      5:4      Major Third      Material gate
   +2      4:3      Perfect Fourth   Material flow
   +3      3:2      Perfect Fifth    Material storage
   +4      2:1      Octave           Material pole
```

### What Bridges Each Interval

Each symmetric pair has "bridge planets" that appear in **both** domains:

| Interval | Positions | Bridge Planets | Character |
|----------|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Octave** | -4 ↔ +4 | Venus | Pure stability |
| **Perfect Fifth** | -3 ↔ +3 | Venus | Harmonic anchor |
| **Perfect Fourth** | -2 ↔ +2 | Sun, Mars | Dynamic action |
| **Major Third** | -1 ↔ +1 | Pluto, Uranus | Transformation gate |

### The Pattern

- **Venus** bridges the **most consonant** intervals (octave, fifth)
- **Sun/Mars** bridge the **action** interval (fourth)
- **Pluto/Uranus** bridge the **gate** intervals (third)

This isn't random. Each planet occupies intervals that match its harmonic function.
