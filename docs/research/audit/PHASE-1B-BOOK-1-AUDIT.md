### Chapter 2: The Eight Positions

**DERIVED:**
- Eight trigrams map to eight positions: 4 on each side of zero
- **Poles (+/-4):** Heaven (111) and Earth (000) — maximum displacement
- **Storage (+/-3):** Lake (110) and Mountain (001) — energy accumulated
- **Flow (+/-2):** Fire (101) and Water (010) — active movement (voltage/current)
- **Threshold (+/-1):** Wind (011) and Thunder (100) — gates of transformation
- Each pair sums to 111 (binary complement principle)
- Gate-threshold positions are where transformation between domains occurs

**EMPIRICAL:**
- 3,000 year naming convention aligns with wave mechanics

### Chapter 3: The Four Pairs (AXES)

**DERIVED:**
- Four axes emerge from eight positions:
  - **Poles Axis** (Heaven<->Earth): Complete reference frame
  - **Storage Axis** (Lake<->Mountain): Reservoir function (capacitance/inductance)
  - **Flow Axis** (Fire<->Water): Active movement (voltage/current)
  - **Gates Axis** (Wind<->Thunder): Transform energy between domains
- Each axis sums to 111 (complete energy)

### Chapter 4: The 64 Movements

**DERIVED:**
- 64 hexagrams = 64 possible movements (8 origins x 8 destinations)
- **Standing waves** (8): Inner and outer trigrams identical — holding position
- **Cross-zero movements** (32): Transform between domains
- **Same-side movements** (24): Circulate within a domain
- Hexagrams are vectors with direction — same positions, opposite directions create opposite meanings

---

### Chapter 5: Three Types of Movement

**DERIVED:**
- **Standing waves**: Pure states, anchors, hold position without transformation (8 total)
- **Cross-zero movements**: Energy changes domain, transformation instantaneous
- **Same-side movements**: Circulation within same domain, no fundamental transformation
- Cross-zero movements actually cross the still point (no middle ground)

### Chapter 6: The Six Lines

**DERIVED:**
- Each hexagram's journey unfolds through six stages:
  - Lines 1-3: Inner trigram (origin position), developing toward threshold
  - Line 3: Maximum expression of origin, pressing against threshold
  - **Line 3->4 transition**: The actual crossing (discontinuity)
  - Lines 4-6: Outer trigram (destination), developing toward completion
- Standing waves have no threshold (same position inner and outer)
