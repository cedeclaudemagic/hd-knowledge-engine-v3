### Statistical Consideration

**Expected by chance:** ~30% (gaps 1-8 contain Fibonacci: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8)
**Observed:** 82.9% overall

This appears significant, BUT there's a crucial caveat:

**Small gaps inflate Fibonacci matches.**
- Gaps of 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 are ALL Fibonacci
- Only gaps of 4, 6, 7 are non-Fibonacci in the 1-8 range
- This means 5/8 (62.5%) of small gaps match Fibonacci

The elevated rate (82.9%) is still above this, but less dramatic than the raw comparison suggests.

### Uranus Anomaly

**Uranus** shows the lowest Fibonacci match rate (35.7%), close to random.

This is the OPPOSITE of the golden angle finding, where Uranus showed the HIGHEST enrichment.

This contradiction suggests the phi patterns are not systematic.

The I Ching/Human Design system is built on:
- Binary division (yin/yang)
