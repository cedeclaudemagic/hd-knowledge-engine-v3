The Lunar Nodes are not abstract astrological points. They are **pure geometry** made manifest in the solar system.

### The Intersection of Two Planes

The Moon orbits Earth on a plane tilted approximately 5.1° from the ecliptic (Earth's orbital plane around the Sun). These two planes intersect along a **line**:

```
                    MOON'S ORBITAL PLANE
                         /
                        /
    ═══════════════════●═══════════════════  ECLIPTIC (Earth's orbit)
                      /\
         SOUTH NODE ●/  \● NORTH NODE
                    /    \
                   /      \
              (Descending)  (Ascending)
```

This intersection creates exactly **two points** on the zodiac:
- **North Node (Ascending)**: Where the Moon crosses from below to above the ecliptic
- **South Node (Descending)**: Where the Moon crosses from above to below the ecliptic

The Nodes are not celestial bodies. They are the **geometric consequence** of two orbital planes meeting. Ra called them "the line of geometry" — this is literally true.
