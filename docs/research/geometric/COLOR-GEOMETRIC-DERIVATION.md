## Part II: Geometric Analysis

### Hypothesis A: Colors = Octahedron Vertices

**Proposal:** 6 Colors map to 6 octahedron vertices, same structure as Lines.

**Analysis:**
```
Octahedron vertices: (±1, 0, 0), (0, ±1, 0), (0, 0, ±1)
= 6 vertices = 3 axes × 2 directions
```

**Problem:** If Colors and Lines share the same geometric structure, why distinguish them? Ra explicitly treats them as different dimensions of the 216 cube.

**Verdict:** **REJECTED** — Creates structural redundancy

---

### Hypothesis B: Colors = Cube Faces

**Proposal:** 6 Colors map to 6 cube faces.

**Analysis:**
```
Cube has 6 faces: +X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z
= 3 opposite-face pairs
```

**If pairing is 1-6, 2-5, 3-4 (opposite faces):**
- Color 1 (Fear/Appetite) ↔ Color 6 (Innocence/Light)
- Color 2 (Hope/Taste) ↔ Color 5 (Guilt/Sound)
- Color 3 (Desire/Thirst) ↔ Color 4 (Need/Touch)

**Semantic Opposition Test:**

| Pair | Color A | Color B | Opposition Type |
|------|---------|---------|-----------------|
| 1-6 | Fear ↔ Innocence | Polar opposites | Internal threat ↔ External safety |
| 2-5 | Hope ↔ Guilt | Temporal polarity | Future positive ↔ Past negative |
| 3-4 | Desire ↔ Need | Complementary | Internal want ↔ External require |

**Verdict:** **PARTIAL** — Structure fits, but the semantic logic is weak. The oppositions exist but don't feel geometrically necessary.

---

### Hypothesis C: Colors = Merkaba Tetrahedra Vertices

**Proposal:** Colors 1-3 = Void tetrahedron vertices, Colors 4-6 = Material tetrahedron vertices.

**Analysis:**
```
Each tetrahedron has 4 vertices, not 3.
Cannot map 3 Colors to 4 vertices.
```

**Alternative — Tetrahedra Edges:**
```
Each tetrahedron has 6 edges.
Could select 3 edges from each tetrahedron.
```

But this introduces arbitrary selection — which 3 edges? No clear geometric principle.

**Verdict:** **REJECTED** — Vertex count mismatch; edge selection is arbitrary.

---

### Hypothesis D: Colors = Two Domains × Three Axes

**Proposal:** Colors follow a 2 × 3 structure (2 domains × 3 functional axes), with parallel pairs (1-4, 2-5, 3-6).

**Analysis:**

**The Parallel Pairing Model:**

| Axis | Lower (Internal) | Upper (External) | Theme |
|------|------------------|------------------|-------|
| A | Color 1 (Appetite/Timing) | Color 4 (Touch/Nervous) | RHYTHM |
| B | Color 2 (Taste/Openness) | Color 5 (Sound/Frequency) | RECEPTION |
| C | Color 3 (Thirst/Temperature) | Color 6 (Light/Direction) | ENVIRONMENT |

**Fixing Pattern Coherence:**

| Pair | Fixing A | Fixing B | Shared Pattern |
|------|----------|----------|----------------|
| 1-4 | Consecutive/Alternating | Calm/Nervous | Temporal rhythm |
| 2-5 | Open/Closed | High/Low | Bandwidth/range |
| 3-6 | Hot/Cold | Direct/Indirect | Environmental condition |

**Each parallel pair shares a functional dimension!**

**Geometric Interpretation:**
```
Colors = 2 × 3 structure
       = 2 domains (Internal/External)
       × 3 axes (Rhythm, Reception, Environment)

Lines  = 3 × 2 structure
       = 3 axes (X, Y, Z)
       × 2 directions (+, -)
```

**Verdict:** **VALIDATED** — Best fit with Ra's descriptions and fixing patterns.

## Part III: The Critical Distinction

### Lines vs Colors — Different Structures

| Property | Lines | Colors |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Structure | 3 × 2 | 2 × 3 |
| Pattern | 3 axes × 2 directions | 2 domains × 3 axes |
| Geometry | Octahedron vertices | Parallel triangles |
| Pairing | Opposite (1↔4, 2↔5, 3↔6) | Parallel (1↔4, 2↔5, 3↔6) |
| Nature | Spatial direction | Functional domain |

The 216 cube is NOT three copies of the same 6-fold structure.

```
LINES:  Octahedron (spatial)       → 3 axes × 2 endpoints
COLORS: Triangular prism (polar)   → 2 faces × 3 vertices
TONES:  Cognitive binaries         → 3 centers × 2 orientations
```

Each dimension contributes a DIFFERENT type of 6-fold structure.
