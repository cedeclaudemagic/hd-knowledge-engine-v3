Planets with **CW bias** (Earth, Mars, Neptune, Pluto):

Planets that are **balanced**:

### Possible Interpretation

The CW/CCW bias may relate to how the planet interacts with the wave structure:
- **CW planets** (Earth, Mars, Neptune, Pluto) may "move with" the wheel direction
- **CCW planets** (Saturn) may "resist" the wheel direction
- **Balanced planets** are neutral to wheel direction

However, this is SPECULATIVE. The sample sizes are small and the pattern is not statistically robust.
