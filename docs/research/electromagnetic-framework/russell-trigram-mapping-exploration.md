## The Core Insight

The eight trigrams map onto **four complementary axes**, each representing a fundamental aspect of electromagnetic systems:

1. **POLES (Reference)** — Heaven/Earth — Source and Sink
2. **CONTAINERS (Storage)** — Lake/Mountain — Capacitance and Inductance
3. **FLOW (Exchange)** — Fire/Water — Voltage and Current
4. **GATES (Switch)** — Thunder/Wind — Close/ON and Open/OFF

The wheel can turn both ways, and each axis represents a true electromagnetic duality.

### Axis 2: CONTAINERS — Lake (110) ↔ Mountain (001)

**Energy Storage**

Two fundamental ways to store energy in a circuit:

| Trigram | Binary | EM Function | Quality |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Lake** | 110 | **CAPACITANCE** | Electric field storage, holds voltage, static |
| **Mountain** | 001 | **INDUCTANCE** | Magnetic field storage, maintains current, dynamic |

- **Capacitor** (Lake): Stores energy by **holding still** - charge on plates, separated, waiting
- **Inductor** (Mountain): Stores energy by **keeping moving** - current flowing, field sustained

These are true electromagnetic duals:
- Capacitance resists changes in **voltage**
- Inductance resists changes in **current**
- Energy in capacitor: E = ½CV²
- Energy in inductor: E = ½LI²

**Lake** (110) - open at top, containing below - like a reservoir of charge
**Mountain** (001) - solid foundation, field rising - like a coil building magnetic potential

### Axis 3: FLOW — Fire (101) ↔ Water (010)

**Active Exchange**

Two aspects of electrical flow, related by Ohm's Law (V = IR):

| Trigram | Binary | EM Function | Quality |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Fire** | 101 | **VOLTAGE** | Potential difference, pressure, the push |
| **Water** | 010 | **CURRENT** | Actual flow, movement, the stream |

- **Voltage** (Fire): The pressure that causes flow, radiating outward, the "push"
- **Current** (Water): The actual movement of charge, flowing through, the "stream"

Fire and Water are neighbors to their respective storage mechanisms:
- **Lake → Fire**: Capacitance releases as Voltage (stored charge creates potential difference)
- **Mountain → Water**: Inductance sustains as Current (magnetic field maintains flow)

### Axis 4: GATES — Thunder (100) ↔ Wind (011)

**Switching / Transition**

The control points where state changes occur:

| Trigram | Binary | EM Function | Quality |
|---------|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Thunder** | 100 | **CLOSE / ON** | Circuit completing, discharge event, rising edge |
| **Wind** | 011 | **OPEN / OFF** | Circuit breaking, field sustained, falling edge |

This is where the **AC/DC flip** happens - the commutator function.

**Thunder** (100) - Yang breaking through yin:
- The spark, the lightning strike
- Circuit closes, current flows
- The **collapse** of the field into directed flow
- Rising edge (0→1)

**Wind** (011) - Yin opening under yang:
- The field saturated with charge, hovering
- Circuit open, potential sustained but not flowing
- The **maintenance** of superposition
- Falling edge (1→0)

**The Quantum Connection:**

Thunder/Wind may represent the measurement problem:
- **Wind** = Wave function (field, probability, all possibilities)
- **Thunder** = Collapsed state (discharge, definite outcome, particle)

The act of "closing the gate" (Thunder) is like observation collapsing the wave function.
The "open gate" (Wind) maintains superposition - all possibilities humming in the field.

## The DC/AC Split

A key insight: The **first bit (Line 1)** determines which "mode" the trigram operates in:

**DC Side (Line 1 = Yang):**
- Heaven: 111
- Lake: 110
- Fire: 101
- Thunder: 100

**AC Side (Line 1 = Yin):**
- Wind: 011
- Water: 010
- Mountain: 001
- Earth: 000

- **DC** has a stable yang foundation - unidirectional, assertive, determined
- **AC** has a receptive yin foundation - oscillating, responsive, alternating

Thunder and Wind sit at the **crossing point** - where one mode becomes the other.

## The Hexagram Crossing Pattern

Looking at hexagram sequences around the wheel, the upper trigram follows a pattern that **reverses** at Thunder/Wind:

**One direction:**
Heaven → Lake → Fire → Thunder → **[CROSSING]** → Wind → Water → Mountain → Earth

**Other direction:**
Earth → Mountain → Water → Wind → **[CROSSING]** → Thunder → Fire → Lake → Heaven

Thunder (100) and Wind (011) are the **zero-crossing points** of the wave - not zero energy, but maximum **rate of change**.

Like in an AC waveform:
- At peaks (Heaven/Earth): maximum amplitude, zero rate of change
- At zero-crossing (Thunder/Wind): zero amplitude, maximum rate of change
