## Part 1: Why Solar Plexus Takes Absolute Priority

### The Electromagnetic Explanation

The Solar Plexus operates at position **-2 (Fire/Voltage)**.

In electromagnetic terms:
- **Voltage** is *potential difference* — energy wanting to discharge
- **Fire** in the I Ching is "clinging" — it attaches, persists, fluctuates
- The Solar Plexus contains Gate 30 (Fire/Fire) — the standing wave at voltage position

**Voltage requires time to stabilise.**

This is the fundamental principle: you cannot measure voltage accurately while it's fluctuating. You must wait for the wave to complete its cycle to know the true potential.

### The Wave Mechanics

```
         Peak (+)
           /\
          /  \
         /    \
        /      \
───────/────────\────────── Neutral (clarity point)
                 \      /
                  \    /
                   \  /
                    \/
                  Trough (-)
```

The emotional wave moves through:
- **Hope** (rising toward peak)
- **Peak** (maximum positive charge)
- **Pain** (falling toward trough)
- **Trough** (maximum negative charge)
- **Return** (rising back toward neutral)

**"There is no truth in the now"** means: any decision made at peak or trough is distorted by the wave position. Only at the neutral point (or after observing the full wave) can the true signal be distinguished from the fluctuation.

### Comparison with Other Centres

| Centre | Position | Why NOT priority over SP? |
|--------|----------|---------------------------|
| Sacral | -4, +1/+2 | Responds in the now — but SP wave distorts even Sacral response |
| Spleen | -1 | Instantaneous — but can be drowned out by emotional noise |
| Heart | +1 | Will-based — but will can be inflated/deflated by wave position |
| G | ±4 | Identity — but identity expression varies with emotional state |

**The wave modulates everything.** When the Solar Plexus is defined, its fluctuation affects all other centres' signals. Therefore it must be waited out first.

## Part 2: Centre-by-Centre Authority Analysis

### Solar Plexus Authority (Emotional)

**Position:** -2 (Voltage/Fire)
**Axis:** Flow
**Standing Wave:** Gate 30 (Clinging Fire)

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Voltage is *potential wanting to discharge*
- Fire "clings" — emotions persist and fluctuate
- The wave is ALWAYS moving (never static when defined)

**Decision mechanism:**
- Wait through the wave cycle
- Observe the question/decision at different wave positions
- Clarity emerges when the same answer appears regardless of wave position

**Why absolute priority:**
Voltage fluctuation affects measurement of ALL other signals. The wave must be accounted for before any other authority can be trusted.

### Sacral Authority

**Position:** -4 (Source/Heaven) + +1/+2 (Gate IN/Current)
**Axis:** Poles + Gates + Flow
**Standing Wave:** Gate 29 (Water/Water - Current)

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Split between Source (-4) and material engagement (+1/+2)
- Generates sustained life force from cosmic source
- Responds through the gate position (+1 Thunder) — in-the-moment engagement

**Decision mechanism:**
- Gut response: sounds (uh-huh, uhn-uhn)
- Response happens NOW — the Sacral doesn't deliberate
- Binary: available energy or not

**Why second priority:**
The Sacral response is instantaneous and binary — but if SP is defined, the wave position can make the Sacral "feel" available when it isn't (at peak) or unavailable when it is (at trough).

### Splenic Authority

**Position:** -1 (Gate OUT/Wind)
**Axis:** Gates
**Standing Wave:** Gate 57 (Wind/Wind - The Gentle)

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Gate OUT position — the discrimination threshold
- Wind is "gentle penetration" — subtle, quiet
- ALL Spleen gates start at -1 — pure gate function

**Decision mechanism:**
- Speaks once, in the moment, very quietly
- Does NOT repeat
- Survival-based: safe/not safe, healthy/unhealthy

**Why third priority:**
The Spleen is the fastest authority — instantaneous discrimination. But:
- If SP is defined, emotional noise can drown out the quiet splenic voice
- If Sacral is defined, the generating motor's response takes precedence (more fundamental life force engagement)

The Spleen operates at the gate threshold (-1) — it discriminates what passes. But it doesn't generate energy (Sacral) or create pressure (SP).

### Ego/Heart Authority

**Position:** +1 (Gate IN/Thunder)
**Axis:** Gates
**Standing Wave:** Gate 51 (Thunder/Thunder - The Arousing)

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Gate IN position — the initiation threshold
- Thunder "arouses" — shock, initiation, willpower
- The Heart pushes energy through the gate into manifestation

**Decision mechanism:**
- "What do I want?"
- Will-based commitment
- Promises and deals

**Why fourth priority:**
The Heart initiates at +1 — it opens the gate. But:
- Will can be distorted by emotional wave (if SP defined)
- Will should respond to life (if Sacral defined) rather than initiate alone
- Will isn't survival-based discrimination (Spleen)

The Heart is powerful but narrow — only 4 gates, and will-based decisions can burn out if overused without the motor's natural rest cycle.

### Self-Projected Authority (G Centre)

**Position:** ±4 (Both poles - Heaven and Earth)
**Axis:** Poles
**Standing Waves:** Gate 1 (Heaven/Heaven) and Gate 2 (Earth/Earth)

**Electromagnetic character:**
- Contains BOTH polar anchors — complete reference frame
- The G is the bodygraph's electromagnetic ground
- Identity and direction emanate from the still point between poles

**Decision mechanism:**
- Hear yourself speak
- Talk through decisions with others (not for their input, but to hear your own voice)
- Identity resonance: does this direction feel like "me"?

**Why fifth priority:**
The G provides the reference frame — but it doesn't generate energy (Sacral), create pressure (SP), discriminate survival (Spleen), or initiate will (Heart).

Self-Projected authority works through the Throat connection (G-Throat channels). Speaking externalises the internal reference, making it audible.

### Mental/Environmental Authority

**Position:** Mixed (Ajna gates span multiple positions)
**Axis:** Multiple
**Standing Waves:** None in Ajna

**Electromagnetic character:**
- The Ajna processes information — it doesn't generate or discriminate
- Outputs to storage (±3) and poles (±4) — conceptual organisation
- No direct authority function — the mind is for others, not self

**Decision mechanism:**
- No internal authority available
- Use others as a sounding board
- The environment (who you talk to, where you are) becomes the authority

**Why sixth priority:**
Mental authority only applies when NO other authority is available (Head/Ajna defined, nothing else). The mind processes but doesn't know — it can rationalise any position.

This is actually "no inner authority" — the person must rely on external resonance.

### Lunar Authority (Reflector)

**Position:** None (no defined centres)
**Axis:** None
**Standing Waves:** None

**Electromagnetic character:**
- No consistent internal electromagnetic signature
- The Reflector samples the entire field
- Moon's 28-day cycle moves through all 64 gates

**Decision mechanism:**
- Wait 28 days (full lunar cycle)
- Sample the decision from every possible gate/channel configuration
- Clarity emerges from the pattern across the whole cycle

**Why seventh (last) priority:**
Reflectors have no internal authority at all. Their authority IS time — specifically, lunar time. The Moon's transit through all gates creates a complete sampling of how the decision feels from every electromagnetic position.

## Part 3: The Temporal Dimension

### Authority and Time

| Authority | Time Required | Why |
|-----------|--------------|-----|
| Emotional | Hours to days | Wave must complete cycle(s) |
| Sacral | Instantaneous | Binary response in the now |
| Splenic | Instantaneous | One-time signal, doesn't repeat |
| Ego | Variable | Commitment requires checking willpower capacity |
| Self-Projected | Minutes to hours | Must speak and hear self |
| Mental | Variable | Depends on finding right sounding board |
| Lunar | 28 days | Full moon cycle required |

### The Flow Axis and Time

The Flow axis (Fire ↔ Water, -2 ↔ +2) represents **active exchange** — energy in motion.

- **Voltage (-2)** is potential that fluctuates — requires TIME to stabilise
- **Current (+2)** is flow that sustains — operates in continuous present

This is why the Solar Plexus (Voltage) requires waiting, while the Sacral (which includes Current via Gate 29) responds immediately.

### The Gate Axis and Instantaneity

The Gate axis (Wind ↔ Thunder, -1 ↔ +1) represents **switching** — binary states.

- **Gate OUT (-1)** discriminates: pass/don't pass — instantaneous
- **Gate IN (+1)** initiates: open/close — commitment-based

Both Spleen (-1) and Heart (+1) operate at the gate threshold — they don't require time in the same way as the Flow axis. But the Spleen's discrimination is survival-based (more fundamental), while the Heart's initiation is will-based (requires resources).

## Part 4: The Three Awareness Centres

Human Design has three awareness centres, each with different temporal characteristics:

| Centre | Type | Position | Temporal Mode | Function |
|--------|------|----------|---------------|----------|
| Solar Plexus | Awareness/Motor | -2 | Wave over time | Emotional truth |
| Spleen | Awareness | -1 | Instantaneous | Survival truth |
| Ajna | Awareness | Mixed | Conceptual (no authority) | Mental processing |

### Why Solar Plexus Dominates

The Solar Plexus is BOTH awareness AND motor:
- As awareness: it perceives emotional truth
- As motor: it generates pressure (voltage wanting to discharge)

This dual function is unique. The Spleen is pure awareness (no motor function). The Ajna is pure processing (no motor, no direct authority).

The Solar Plexus's motor function creates the wave. The wave affects everything. Therefore its awareness function (emotional clarity) must be waited for before other authorities can be trusted.

### The Spleen's Quiet Voice

The Spleen operates at -1 (Gate OUT) — the discrimination threshold.

Key characteristics:
- Speaks once, doesn't repeat
- Very quiet — can be overridden by louder signals
- Survival-based — is this safe/healthy RIGHT NOW?

The Spleen's instantaneity is its power and its vulnerability. If you miss it, it's gone. If emotional noise is present, it can be drowned out.

### The Ajna's Non-Authority

The Ajna processes information but has no authority function because:
- It doesn't generate energy (no motor)
- It doesn't operate at a gate threshold (no discrimination/initiation)
- It outputs to storage and poles — organisation, not decision

The mind is "the passenger, not the driver." It can rationalise any position after the fact, but it cannot know what's correct for the self.

## Part 5: Motor Centres and Authority

Four motor centres exist, but only two have direct authority function:

| Motor | Authority? | Why/Why Not |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Solar Plexus | YES (#1) | Awareness + Motor — emotional wave |
| Sacral | YES (#2) | Pure generation — life force response |
| Heart | YES (#4) | Will/commitment — when no higher authority |
| Root | NO | Pressure only — no awareness, no decision function |

### Why Root Has No Authority

The Root operates at ±3 (Storage) — capacitance and inductance.

- It generates PRESSURE (adrenal fuel)
- It doesn't discriminate, respond, or commit
- Its function is to DRIVE, not DECIDE

Root pressure pushes energy into the system but provides no information about what's correct. It's fuel, not guidance.

### The Sacral's Unique Position

The Sacral spans multiple positions:
- Four gates at -4 (Heaven/Source): 5, 9, 14, 34
- Five gates at +1/+2 (Thunder/Water): 3, 27, 29, 42, 59

This split between SOURCE and ENGAGEMENT is significant:
- The Sacral draws from cosmic source (-4)
- The Sacral responds in material engagement (+1/+2)

The response mechanism uses the +1/+2 gates — in-the-moment engagement with what's presented. But the sustained capacity comes from the -4 gates — connection to source.

Reflectors have no defined centres. They have only:

No other authority is possible because no centres are defined.

## Conclusion

The Authority hierarchy is electromagnetic:

1. **Wave positions require time** (Solar Plexus at -2)
2. **Source positions generate** (Sacral at -4)
3. **Gate positions discriminate/initiate** (Spleen at -1, Heart at +1)
4. **Polar positions reference** (G at ±4)
5. **No position = no inner authority** (Mental, Lunar)

The order follows the principle: **what modulates other signals must be accounted for first.**

Solar Plexus voltage fluctuation affects ALL other signals. Therefore it has absolute priority. Each subsequent authority becomes available only when the higher-priority centre is undefined.
