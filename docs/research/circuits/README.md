## Key Findings

### Integration Circuit
- The only circuit with **no tribal or collective purpose** - purely individual
- Venus exalts strongly (3x at Gate 57)
- Moon fails consistently (3x detriment at Gate 57)
- Self-coherence cannot tolerate emotional fluctuation

### Understanding Circuit (Logic)
- Pattern recognition and formula sharing
- Saturn and structure serve this circuit
- Needs completion before sharing

### Sensing Circuit (Abstract)
- Experiential processing through the wave
- Sun exalts strongly in this circuit
- Emotional wave must complete for wisdom

### Knowing Circuit (Individual)
- Unique mutation and knowing
- Can feel alien/isolating
- Must wait for recognition

### Tribal Circuits (Defense, Ego, Centering)
- Support, resources, and will
- Mars has complex relationship - sometimes serves, sometimes corrupts
- Deals and bargains central to function
