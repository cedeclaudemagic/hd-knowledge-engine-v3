## Key Discoveries

### 1. No Pure Standing Wave Channels

No channel connects two doubled hexagrams. Every channel has at least one gate with a **vector** — movement, not just being.

### 2. The "Transformer + Circulator" Pattern

The most common pattern (21 of 36 channels) pairs a **cross-zero** gate with a **same-phase** gate:
- The transformer gate provides **transformation potential**
- The circulator gate provides **grounding** (either in matter or void)

This suggests channels function as **regulated transformers** — the circulator moderates the transformer's intensity.

### 3. Double Transformers Are Rare But Significant

Only 7 channels pair two cross-zero gates. These are **high-intensity** configurations:
- 6-59 (Mating): Both dematerialising from current
- 21-45 (Money Line): Both crossing at different amplitudes
- 35-36 (Transitoriness): Perfect mirror — manifesting + dematerialising through same positions
- 47-64 (Abstraction): Both dematerialising — confusion meeting oppression

### 4. The 35-36 Mirror

Channel 35-36 is unique: Gate 35 (+4→-2) and Gate 36 (-2→+4) are **exact mirrors**. One manifests, one dematerialises, through the same positions. The channel name "Transitoriness" perfectly captures this — energy flowing both ways simultaneously.

### 5. Gate 10's Multiple Channels

Gate 10 (Treading, -3→-4, Void Circulation) participates in THREE channels:
- 10-20 (Awakening)
- 10-34 (Exploration)
- 10-57 (Perfected Form)

All three pair it with different transformation types, suggesting Gate 10 is a **versatile interface** in the void domain.

### 6. Gate 57's Standing Wave Hub

Gate 57 (The Gentle, -1, Pure Gate OUT) participates in THREE channels:
- 10-57 (Perfected Form)
- 20-57 (Brainwave)
- 34-57 (Power)

As a pure standing wave at the gate position, it acts as a **stable reference** for transformation channels.
