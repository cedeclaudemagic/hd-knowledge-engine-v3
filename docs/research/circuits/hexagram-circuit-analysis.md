## Three Classes of Hexagram

### 1. Same-Phase Hexagrams
Both trigrams on the same side of zero (both + or both -).
- Energy circulates within material realm OR within void
- No zero-crossing required
- 32 hexagrams (16 all-positive, 16 all-negative)

### 2. Cross-Phase Hexagrams
Trigrams on opposite sides of zero.
- Energy must transit through the Monopole
- **Transformative configurations** - matter ↔ field
- 32 hexagrams (16 materializing, 16 dematerializing)

### 3. Diagonal Hexagrams
The 8 doubled trigrams (Heaven/Heaven, Earth/Earth, etc.)
- Pure states, no vector
- Standing waves rather than travelling waves
