## Part 1: The Four Axes Framework

### Trigram-to-Position Mapping

The eight trigrams occupy positions on a wave from -4 (void/source) to +4 (matter/sink), with zero as the Monopole (still point).

| Position | Trigram | Binary | Family | Axis | EM Function |
|----------|---------|--------|--------|------|-------------|
| **-4** | Heaven | 111 | Father | POLES | Source / Void |
| -3 | Lake | 110 | Youngest Daughter | STORAGE | Capacitance |
| -2 | Fire | 101 | Middle Daughter | FLOW | Voltage |
| **-1** | Wind | 011 | Eldest Daughter | GATES | Gate OUT / Open |
| **0** | — | — | — | CENTER | Monopole / Still Point |
| **+1** | Thunder | 100 | Eldest Son | GATES | Gate IN / Close |
| +2 | Water | 010 | Middle Son | FLOW | Current |
| +3 | Mountain | 001 | Youngest Son | STORAGE | Inductance |
| **+4** | Earth | 000 | Mother | POLES | Sink / Carbon |

### The Four Axes

| Axis | Trigrams | Positions | EM Function | Relationship |
|------|----------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Poles** | Heaven ↔ Earth | -4 ↔ +4 | Source ↔ Sink | Reference frame |
| **Storage** | Lake ↔ Mountain | -3 ↔ +3 | Capacitance ↔ Inductance | Energy storage |
| **Flow** | Fire ↔ Water | -2 ↔ +2 | Voltage ↔ Current | Active exchange |
| **Gates** | Wind ↔ Thunder | -1 ↔ +1 | Open ↔ Close | Switching |

### Binary Complement Pattern

Each axis pair sums to 111 (Heaven):
- Thunder (100) + Wind (011) = 111
- Water (010) + Fire (101) = 111
- Mountain (001) + Lake (110) = 111

Crossing through zero is binary inversion.

### Observer-Relative Grounding

- **Earth (+4)** = Local ground (carbon, matter, where we observe from)
- **Heaven (-4)** = Absolute ground (void, source, cosmic reference)
- **Zero** = The Monopole, not a position but the axis everything rotates around

## Part 2: Gate Classification

Every hexagram (gate) consists of two trigrams: lower (inner) and upper (outer). The relationship between their positions determines gate type.

### Three Gate Types

| Type | Definition | Count | Function |
|------|------------|-------|----------|
| **Cross-zero** | Inner and outer on opposite sides of zero | 32 | Transformation |
| **Same-phase** | Inner and outer on same side of zero | 24 | Circulation |
| **Doubled** | Inner and outer are identical | 8 | Standing wave |

### Cross-Zero Gates (32)

Energy crosses the Monopole — fundamental transformation between void and matter.

**Manifesting (16):** Negative inner → Positive outer (void → matter)
**Dematerialising (16):** Positive inner → Negative outer (matter → void)

### Same-Phase Gates (24)

Energy circulates within a domain — no transformation.

**Material (+):** 12 gates circulating within matter
**Void (-):** 12 gates circulating within potential

### Doubled Gates (8)

## Part 3: The Six Lines

Each hexagram's six lines map to stages of transit between inner and outer trigram.

### Line Structure

```
Line 6: ─────────── Outer trigram, line 3 (completion)
Line 5: ─────────── Outer trigram, line 2 (development)
Line 4: ─────────── Outer trigram, line 1 (entry)
        ═══════════ THRESHOLD (3→4 junction)
Line 3: ─────────── Inner trigram, line 3 (completion)
Line 2: ─────────── Inner trigram, line 2 (development)
Line 1: ─────────── Inner trigram, line 1 (entry)
```

### The 3→4 Threshold

The transition from line 3 to line 4 marks the shift from inner to outer trigram. In cross-zero gates, this is where energy passes through the Monopole.

### Transition Quality by Gate Type

| Gate Type | 3→4 Transition | Character |

## Part 4: Channel Pairing Types

Channels connect two gates. The combination of gate types determines channel function.

### Pairing Categories

| Pairing | Components | Function |
|---------|------------|----------|
| **Double Transformer** | Cross-zero + Cross-zero | High-intensity transformation |
| **Transformer + Circulator** | Cross-zero + Same-phase | Regulated transformation |
| **Transformer + Standing Wave** | Cross-zero + Doubled | Anchored transformation |
| **Double Circulator** | Same-phase + Same-phase | Cross-domain circulation |
| **Circulator + Standing Wave** | Same-phase + Doubled | Anchored circulation |

### Special Patterns

**Oscillators:** Double transformers with opposite directions — cycle continuously
**Parallel transformers:** Double transformers with same direction — push energy one way
**Commutators:** Transformers that meet at the gate boundary (±1) — switch states

## Part 7: Two Change Mechanisms

The framework contains two distinct mechanisms for change:

### Transformation vs Mutation

| Aspect | Transformation | Mutation |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| **Mechanism** | Energy crossing zero | Phase alignment |
| **Character** | Continuous, gradual | Sudden, pulse-like |
| **Experience** | Flowing transition | On/off switching |
| **Predictability** | Trackable | Unpredictable |
| **Architecture** | High transformation % | Low transformation + high anchoring |
| **Example** | Sensing (79%) | Knowing (28%) |

### Why Both Exist

- **Transformation** handles change that needs to be processed through (experiences, patterns)
- **Mutation** handles change that arrives suddenly (knowing, inspiration)

Different functions require different change mechanisms, hence different architectures.
