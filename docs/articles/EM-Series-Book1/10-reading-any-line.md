## The Five Layers

Every line exists within five nested contexts:

**Layer 1: The Gate**
What type of movement is this hexagram? Standing wave, cross-zero, or same-side? What positions does it span?

**Layer 2: The Line**
Where in the journey does this line sit? Lines 1-3 are in the origin (inner trigram). Lines 4-6 are in the destination (outer trigram). Line 3 presses against the threshold. Line 4 has just crossed.

**Layer 3: The Channel**
What gate does this one pair with? What pairing type do they create — double transformer, transformer-circulator, transformer-anchor, double circulator, or circulator-anchor?

**Layer 4: The Circuit**
Which circuit does this channel belong to? What's the architecture — how many anchors, what positions, what transformation percentage? Mutation or continuous processing?

**Layer 5: The Centre**
Which centre does this gate sit in? What's the centre's electromagnetic signature? How does position clustering shape function?

Reading a line means seeing all five layers at once.

## The Methodology

**Step 1: Identify the gate type**

Look at the hexagram's two trigrams.
- Same trigram inner and outer? → **Standing wave**
- Inner and outer on opposite sides of zero? → **Cross-zero**
- Inner and outer on the same side of zero? → **Same-side**

Note the specific positions: What's the inner trigram position? What's the outer?

**Step 2: Locate the line in the journey**

- Line 1: Entering the origin position
- Line 2: Developing in the origin
- Line 3: Completing the origin, pressing toward threshold
- Line 4: Just crossed into destination, finding footing
- Line 5: Developing in the destination
- Line 6: Completing the destination, culmination

For standing waves, there's no threshold — lines 1-3 and 4-6 express the same position from inner and outer perspectives.

For cross-zero gates, the 3→4 transition is transformation — domain change.

For same-side gates, the 3→4 transition is mode shift — development without domain change.

**Step 3: Consider the channel context**

What gate pairs with this one? What's the pairing type?

- Double transformer: Maximum intensity — is this gate part of an oscillator or parallel driver?
- Transformer + circulator: Regulated transformation — does this gate transform or regulate?
- Transformer + anchor: Grounded transformation — does this gate transform or anchor?
- Double circulator: Domain bridging — which domain does this gate circulate in?
- Circulator + anchor: Grounded circulation — does this gate circulate or anchor?

**Step 4: See the circuit architecture**

Which circuit contains this channel? What's the signature?

- Knowing (3 anchors): Phase-lock, mutation, sudden knowing
- Understanding (2 storage anchors): Pattern accumulation, logical building
- Integration (1 gate-out anchor): Self-discrimination, boundary maintenance
- Centering (1 gate-in anchor): Initiation, direction, shock
- Defense (0 anchors): Material grounding, protection, nurturance
- Ego (0 anchors): Material exchange, resources, will
- Sensing (2 flow anchors): Continuous processing, emotional wave, experience

**Step 5: Feel the centre signature**

Which centre houses this gate? What's its electromagnetic profile?

- Spleen: All gates at -1 — pure discrimination
- Throat: All gates in form — pure expression
- G Centre: Both poles — complete reference
- Solar Plexus: Voltage dominant — emotional pressure
- Sacral: Current emphasis — life force flow
- Root: Storage positions — accumulated pressure
- Heart: Gate-in anchor — will into form
- Head: High transformation — mental pressure
- Ajna: Void emphasis — conceptual processing

## Worked Example: Gate 57, Line 4

Let's apply the methodology.

**Layer 1: The Gate**

Gate 57 is Wind over Wind. Position -1 to position -1.

Same trigram inner and outer → **Standing wave**

This gate holds position. It doesn't move. It anchors at the gate-out threshold — the point of discrimination where form meets potential.

**Layer 2: The Line**

Line 4 is the first line of the outer trigram. In a moving hexagram, this would be "just crossed the threshold." But in a standing wave, there's no crossing.

Instead, Line 4 represents the shift from *inner* expression of position -1 to *outer* expression of position -1. Same position, but now relating outward rather than inward.

Lines 1-3 of Gate 57: Inner experience of discrimination. The gate-threshold awareness turned inward. Intuition as self-sensing.

Lines 4-6 of Gate 57: Outer expression of discrimination. The gate-threshold awareness turned outward. Intuition as world-sensing.

Line 4 specifically: The pivot. Where inner discrimination begins to express outwardly. The intuitive clarity that starts to direct rather than just sense.

**Layer 3: The Channel**

Gate 57 pairs with Gate 20 to form Channel 57-20, the Brainwave.

Gate 57: Standing wave at -1
Gate 20: Cross-zero dematerialising (+4 → -1)

Pairing type: **Transformer + Anchor**

And notice: Gate 20 is moving *toward* -1. Gate 57 anchors *at* -1. The anchor sits at the transformer's destination. Gate 57 provides the stable arrival point for Gate 20's release of form.

For Line 4 of Gate 57: This line is where the anchor begins to express outwardly — where it starts receiving what Gate 20 delivers. The discrimination that catches the insight as it arrives.

**Layer 4: The Circuit**

Channel 57-20 belongs to the **Integration Circuit**.

Integration has one anchor — and it's Gate 57 itself. This gate is the circuit's anchor.

Integration signature: Commutator architecture. Self-coherence through discrimination. The boundary of the individual.

Line 4 of Gate 57 sits in the anchor of the Integration Circuit. It's not just any discrimination — it's the discrimination that maintains self. The intuitive clarity that knows what's self and what's not-self, now expressing outwardly.

**Layer 5: The Centre**

Gate 57 sits in the **Spleen Centre**.

Spleen signature: ALL gates at -1. Pure discrimination.

This gate is quintessentially Spleen. It's not just in the Spleen — it's a standing wave at the exact position that defines the Spleen's function. Gate 57 *is* the Spleen's deepest nature: discrimination at the threshold, held stable.

**The Interpretation**

Gate 57, Line 4:

A standing wave at the discrimination threshold, shifting from inner to outer expression. The anchor of the Integration Circuit — the self-boundary function. Sitting in the Spleen where all gates discriminate. Paired with Gate 20 as the arrival point for contemplative insight.

This line is: *Intuitive clarity turning outward. The moment discrimination stops being private sensing and starts being directive knowing. The anchor expressing. Self-coherence made visible.*

Traditional keynote for 57.4: "The Director" — one who guides others through clarity.

The electromagnetic reading arrives at the same place: discrimination (gate-threshold position) expressing outwardly (Line 4) as direction (Integration Circuit's self-coherence now applied externally).

## The Pattern

Notice what happens when you read the layers:

- The gate type tells you *what kind* of movement (or non-movement)
- The line position tells you *where in the journey*
- The channel context tells you *how this gate relates to its pair*
- The circuit architecture tells you *what mode of change* (mutation, processing, accumulation)
- The centre signature tells you *what function* this serves

The interpretation isn't invented. It emerges. The layers constrain and inform each other until the meaning becomes clear.

And the meaning aligns with traditional interpretations — because the traditional interpretations were encoding electromagnetic reality. The ancients knew what they were mapping. We're just seeing the structure they embedded.
