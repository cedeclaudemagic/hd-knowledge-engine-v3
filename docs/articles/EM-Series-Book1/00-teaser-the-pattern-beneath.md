## The Positions

Imagine the wave. At the centre is zero — the still point. On one side: the domain of **potential** (the unmanifest, the possible, the void). On the other: the domain of **form** (the manifest, the actual, the crystallised).

Now place the eight trigrams:

**Heaven** sits at the far end of potential. Maximum void. Pure source. Binary code: 111.

**Earth** sits at the far end of form. Maximum density. Pure sink. Binary code: 000.

Notice: 111 and 000. Complete opposites. Every bit flipped. The poles of the wave.

**Thunder** sits just past zero, on the form side. The threshold where potential *enters* manifestation. The gate in. Binary: 100.

**Wind** sits just past zero, on the potential side. The threshold where form *dissolves* back toward potential. The gate out. Binary: 011.

Again: 100 and 011. Complete opposites. The gates of transformation.

**Fire** sits mid-range in the potential domain. Voltage. Pressure. The push that drives. Binary: 101.

**Water** sits mid-range in the form domain. Current. Flow. Energy in motion. Binary: 010.

Opposites again: 101 and 010. The flow axis.

**Lake** sits near the potential pole. Storage. Charge held ready to release. Binary: 110.

**Mountain** sits near the form pole. Storage. Pattern crystallised and maintained. Binary: 001.

And once more: 110 and 001. The storage axis.

Eight positions. Four pairs of opposites. Each pair summing to 111. Each pair defining an axis through zero.

This isn't poetry. It's mathematics.

## The Movements

A trigram is a position. A hexagram is *two* trigrams — a **movement** from one position to another.

The lower trigram is where you start. The upper trigram is where you're heading.

Now Hexagram 11 makes sense:

**Peace (泰)**: Earth below, Heaven above.
- Lower trigram: Earth (+4, form pole) — where you begin
- Upper trigram: Heaven (-4, potential pole) — where you're heading
- Movement: From maximum form toward maximum potential

The entire crystallised structure opening back toward pure possibility. Form breathing back toward void. *Of course* that's peace. It's the exhale. The release.

And Hexagram 12:

**Standstill (否)**: Heaven below, Earth above.
- Lower trigram: Heaven (-4, potential pole) — where you begin
- Upper trigram: Earth (+4, form pole) — where you're heading
- Movement: From maximum potential toward maximum form

Pure possibility collapsing directly into maximum density. Nothing flowing, nothing breathing — just sudden crystallisation. *Of course* that's standstill. It's rigidity. Stagnation.

Same positions. Opposite directions. Opposite meanings.

**The direction determines the meaning.**

## What This Unlocks

Once you see hexagrams as movements, everything becomes readable.

The 64 hexagrams fall into three types:

**Standing waves** (8 hexagrams): Same trigram above and below. Not moving — *being*. Pure states held stable.

**Cross-zero movements** (32 hexagrams): Starting on one side of zero, ending on the other. Transformation. Energy actually changing domain.

**Same-side movements** (24 hexagrams): Starting and ending in the same domain. Circulation. Development without fundamental transformation.

Each type has its own character. Standing waves anchor. Cross-zero hexagrams transform. Same-side hexagrams develop.

And the six lines within each hexagram? They're stages of the journey:
- Lines 1-3: Still in the origin position, developing toward threshold
- Lines 4-6: Now in the destination, developing toward completion
- The transition from Line 3 to Line 4: Where the crossing actually happens

When you read a line, you're reading a specific stage of a specific type of movement between specific positions on the wave.

The interpretation isn't arbitrary. It's geometric.
