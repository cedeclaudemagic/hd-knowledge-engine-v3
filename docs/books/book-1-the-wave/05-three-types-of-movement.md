# Three Types of Movement

*Fifth in a series exploring the electromagnetic structure of consciousness*

---

## Where We Are

We've learned that hexagrams are movements — vectors from one position on the wave to another. Inner trigram is origin. Outer trigram is destination. The journey between them is the hexagram's meaning.

And we've identified three families:

- **Standing waves** — same position, no movement
- **Cross-zero** — transformation between domains
- **Same-side** — circulation within a domain

Now we go deeper. What actually *happens* in each type? What kind of change does each produce? Why does it matter which family a hexagram belongs to?

---

## The Standing Waves: Being Without Becoming

There are eight standing waves. Eight hexagrams where the inner and outer trigram are identical. Eight positions held without movement.

| Hexagram | Trigram | Position | Name |
|----------|---------|----------|------|
| 1 | Heaven/Heaven | -4 | The Creative |
| 2 | Earth/Earth | +4 | The Receptive |
| 51 | Thunder/Thunder | +1 | The Arousing |
| 29 | Water/Water | +2 | The Abysmal |
| 52 | Mountain/Mountain | +3 | Keeping Still |
| 57 | Wind/Wind | -1 | The Gentle |
| 30 | Fire/Fire | -2 | The Clinging |
| 58 | Lake/Lake | -3 | The Joyous |

One at each position. Eight anchors on the wave.

What does it mean to have a hexagram that doesn't move?

**Standing waves are pure states.** They're not going anywhere because they're fully *being* somewhere. They don't transform because transformation requires movement. They don't develop because development requires direction.

Instead, they **hold**.

Think of a tuning fork. Strike it, and it vibrates at a single frequency. It doesn't slide up or down the scale. It doesn't modulate. It holds one note, purely.

That's what standing waves do. They hold one position on the wave, purely. They're reference points. Anchors. The fixed notes against which all other movements can be measured.

**The Creative** (Heaven/Heaven) holds pure potential at the source. Not creating yet — just *being* creative. The eternal origin.

**The Receptive** (Earth/Earth) holds pure form at the sink. Not receiving yet — just *being* receptive. The eternal ground.

**The Arousing** (Thunder/Thunder) holds the gate into form. The perpetual threshold of manifestation. Always arriving.

**The Gentle** (Wind/Wind) holds the gate out of form. The perpetual threshold of dissolution. Always releasing.

**The Clinging** (Fire/Fire) holds voltage in potential. Perpetual pressure. Always radiating.

**The Abysmal** (Water/Water) holds current in form. Perpetual flow. Always moving.

**The Joyous** (Lake/Lake) holds charge in potential. Perpetual readiness. Always about to overflow.

**Keeping Still** (Mountain/Mountain) holds pattern in form. Perpetual structure. Always enduring.

Standing waves don't tell you where to go. They tell you what it means to *be here*.

## The Cross-Zero Movements: Transformation

Thirty-two hexagrams cross zero. They start on one side of the wave and end on the other. They pass through the still point. They transform.

This is the most profound type of movement because it changes the fundamental nature of what it carries.

Energy in the potential domain is unmanifest. Possible. Available. Not yet real.

Energy in the form domain is manifest. Actual. Committed. Real.

When energy crosses zero, it changes state. Like water becoming ice, or ice becoming steam. Same substance, different mode of being.

**Direction determines everything.**

---

### Manifesting: Potential → Form

Sixteen hexagrams move from negative positions to positive positions. They cross zero in the direction of crystallisation. Possibility becoming actuality. The unmanifest taking shape.

**Hexagram 11: Peace (泰)**

Inner: Heaven ☰ (position -4, the source)
Outer: Earth ☷ (position +4, the sink)
Movement: -4 → +4

The full journey from potential pole to form pole. Pure possibility moving into pure structure.

Traditional meaning: Peace. Harmony. Heaven and Earth in communion.

In the hexagram image, Heaven is below and Earth is above. The light naturally rises; the heavy naturally descends. They move *toward* each other — meeting, communing, embracing. This is manifestation as willing participation: potential joyfully entering form.

Peace isn't just the geometry of manifestation. It's manifestation welcomed.

**Hexagram 5: Waiting (需)**

Inner: Heaven ☰ (position -4)
Outer: Water ☵ (position +2)
Movement: -4 → +2

From the source of potential toward current in form. Creative power moving toward active manifestation — but not yet arrived at full crystallisation.

Traditional meaning: Waiting. Nourishment. Patience before crossing the great water.

The movement explains it. Energy is travelling from source toward form, but the destination is *flow* (+2), not *pole* (+4). This is manifestation in process, not manifestation complete. Hence: waiting. The crossing is happening, but there's more journey ahead.

**Hexagram 26: Great Taming (大畜)**

Inner: Heaven ☰ (position -4)
Outer: Mountain ☶ (position +3)
Movement: -4 → +3

From the source of potential toward storage in form. Creative power being gathered, accumulated, held in pattern.

Traditional meaning: Great accumulation. Restraining the great. Holding firm.

Pure potential (-4) moving into structured holding (+3). This is power being *tamed* — not released into action but collected into form that endures. The movement captures the meaning perfectly: from maximum possibility to stable containment.

### Dematerialising: Form → Potential

Sixteen hexagrams move from positive positions to negative positions. They cross zero in the direction of dissolution. Actuality reopening to possibility. The manifest releasing back into field.

**Hexagram 12: Standstill (否)**

Inner: Earth ☷ (position +4, the sink)
Outer: Heaven ☰ (position -4, the source)
Movement: +4 → -4

The full journey from form pole to potential pole. Maximum structure moving toward pure possibility.

Traditional meaning: Standstill. Stagnation. Heaven and Earth not communicating.

In the hexagram image, Earth is below and Heaven is above. The heavy naturally sinks further; the light naturally rises further. They move *apart* — separating, disconnecting, refusing contact. Form won't release willingly; potential won't descend to meet it.

Standstill isn't just the geometry of dematerialisation. It's dematerialisation refused.

**Hexagram 20: Contemplation (觀)**

Inner: Earth ☷ (position +4)
Outer: Wind ☴ (position -1)
Movement: +4 → -1

From the form pole toward the gate out. Maximum crystallisation moving toward the threshold of dissolution.

Traditional meaning: Contemplation. Viewing. The sight of the sacred.

Standing in solid form (+4), looking toward the place where form becomes subtle (-1). This is the contemplative gaze — fully embodied but oriented toward what lies beyond embodiment. The view from matter toward mystery.

**Hexagram 35: Progress (晉)**

Inner: Earth ☷ (position +4)
Outer: Fire ☲ (position -2)
Movement: +4 → -2

From the form pole toward voltage in potential. Structure releasing toward active radiance.

Traditional meaning: Progress. Advancing. The bright sun rising.

Form moving toward fire. The solid becoming radiant. This is progress as *illumination* — not building more structure but releasing structure into light. The sun rises by giving away its substance as radiance.

### The Transformation Moment

Here's what's crucial: when energy crosses zero, it passes through the still point. The place of no position.

This is the moment of transformation. The phase transition. The instant where one state ends and another begins.

You can't cross zero gradually. There's no half-way. You're either in potential or in form. The crossing is instantaneous — a discontinuity, a quantum leap, a threshold that has no width.

This is why cross-zero hexagrams describe fundamental changes. Turning points. Metamorphosis. The energy literally changes its nature as it passes through the still point.

Manifesting: the unformed becomes formed.
Dematerialising: the formed becomes unformed.

Both are transformations. Both cross the still point. Both change the nature of what's moving.

## The Same-Side Movements: Circulation

Twenty-four hexagrams stay on one side of zero. They start and end in the same domain. They move energy without transforming it.

This isn't lesser than crossing zero. It's *different*.

Cross-zero movements change *what* energy is.
Same-side movements change *where* energy is.

Circulation develops, refines, moves, stores, releases — all without changing the fundamental domain. Form stays form but develops. Potential stays potential but prepares.

### Material Circulation: Movement Within Form

Twelve hexagrams circulate entirely within the form domain. All positions positive. Manifested energy moving between different states of manifestation.

**Hexagram 23: Splitting Apart (剝)**

Inner: Earth ☷ (position +4)
Outer: Mountain ☶ (position +3)
Movement: +4 → +3

From form pole to form storage. Maximum crystallisation differentiating into held pattern.

Traditional meaning: Splitting apart. Stripping away. The bed stripped to the frame.

Form at its densest (+4) begins to separate into structure (+3). The monolithic becomes articulated. What was solid mass becomes organised pattern. Still form — but developing internal differentiation.

**Hexagram 24: Return (復)**

Inner: Thunder ☳ (position +1)
Outer: Earth ☷ (position +4)
Movement: +1 → +4

From form gate to form pole. Fresh manifestation consolidating toward maximum crystallisation.

Traditional meaning: Return. The turning point. One yang returns at the bottom.

Something has just crossed into form (it's at the gate, +1). Now it journeys toward full establishment (+4). This is the new arrival consolidating, the fresh manifestation taking root, the just-born becoming fully embodied.

**Hexagram 3: Difficulty at the Beginning (屯)**

Inner: Thunder ☳ (position +1)
Outer: Water ☵ (position +2)
Movement: +1 → +2

From form gate to form flow. Fresh manifestation beginning to move.

Traditional meaning: Difficulty at the beginning. The blade of grass pushing through earth.

Just arrived in form (+1), beginning to flow (+2). The first movements after manifestation. Hence the difficulty — everything is new, nothing is established, the current has just started. But it *is* moving. Form is developing.

### Void Circulation: Movement Within Potential

Twelve hexagrams circulate entirely within the potential domain. All positions negative. Unmanifest energy moving between different states of possibility.

**Hexagram 43: Breakthrough (夬)**

Inner: Heaven ☰ (position -4)
Outer: Lake ☱ (position -3)
Movement: -4 → -3

From potential pole to potential storage. Pure source gathering into held charge.

Traditional meaning: Breakthrough. Determination. The lake risen to heaven.

Source potential (-4) concentrating into charged readiness (-3). This is energy gathering itself, preparing, building toward release. The breakthrough is coming — you can feel the charge accumulating — but it hasn't crossed into form yet. Still in potential, but pressurised.

**Hexagram 44: Coming to Meet (姤)**

Inner: Wind ☴ (position -1)
Outer: Heaven ☰ (position -4)
Movement: -1 → -4

From potential gate to potential pole. The dissolved returning fully to source.

Traditional meaning: Coming to meet. The unexpected encounter. One yin appears at the bottom.

Something that released from form (-1, the gate out) travels all the way back to source (-4). Complete return to origin. Not manifesting — the opposite: fully returning to the unmanifest. Coming to meet the source.

**Hexagram 9: Small Taming (小畜)**

Inner: Heaven ☰ (position -4)
Outer: Wind ☴ (position -1)
Movement: -4 → -1

From potential pole toward potential gate. Source moving toward the threshold of manifestation.

Traditional meaning: Small taming. The taming power of the small. Clouds gathering but no rain.

Creative potential (-4) approaching the gate into form (-1) — but not crossing. Still in potential. The clouds gather (energy moves toward the threshold) but haven't discharged (haven't crossed zero). Hence: small taming. The restraint isn't forceful — it's just that the threshold hasn't been crossed *yet*.

## The Three Languages

Each type of movement speaks differently.

**Standing wave language** uses states of being:
- "The Creative *is* creative"
- "Keeping Still *is* still"
- "The Gentle *is* penetrating"

Pure quality. No direction. No change verb. Just presence.

**Cross-zero language** uses transformation words:
- "Becoming," "dissolving"
- "Crystallising," "releasing"
- "Manifesting," "returning to source"

Threshold language. Phase transitions. Something becoming something else.

**Same-side language** uses development words:
- "Building," "accumulating"
- "Preparing," "consolidating"
- "Refining," "differentiating"

Process language. Movement within a domain. Maturation, not metamorphosis.

When you read a hexagram, notice which language it speaks. That tells you what kind of change to expect.

## Why This Matters

When you know a hexagram's type, you know what kind of change it carries.

**Standing wave?** Don't expect movement. Expect to learn what it means to *be* at this position. Let it anchor you. It's a reference point, not a journey.

**Cross-zero?** Expect transformation. Something fundamental is changing. The crossing can't be rushed or avoided. This is metamorphosis — energy changing its essential nature.

**Same-side?** Expect development. Things are moving, but within the same domain. Refinement, not revolution. Growth, not rebirth.

The type isn't interpretation. It's geometry. The shape of the movement determines the shape of the change.
