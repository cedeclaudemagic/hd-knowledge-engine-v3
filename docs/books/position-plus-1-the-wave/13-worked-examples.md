## Example 2: Gate 36, Line 3 — "Transition"

### The Traditional

Gate 36 is "The Darkening of the Light" — emotional crisis that leads to transformation. Line 3 is called "Transition" with themes of moving through the darkest point.

### Layer 1: The Gate

**Gate 36: Fire over Earth**

| Aspect | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Inner Trigram | Fire (-2) |
| Outer Trigram | Earth (+4) |
| Gate Type | **Cross-Zero Manifesting** |
| Movement | From -2 (Voltage) to +4 (Sink) |

Fire (-2) is the origin — voltage, emotional pressure, the realm of feeling. Earth (+4) is the destination — maximum material form, the sink pole. This is cross-zero manifesting: energy moving from void-side toward matter-side, specifically from the pressure position to the densest material position.

The "Darkening of the Light" is the journey from voltage (the fire of spirit, feeling, awareness) into maximum density (earth, the covering, the material). The light "darkens" as it descends from void-side pressure into material-side density — spirit entering matter at its most opaque.

### Layer 2: The Line

**Line 3: Completion of Inner Trigram**

Line 3 completes the inner trigram — the last position before the threshold crossing.

In a cross-zero gate, Line 3 is the **peak of the origin domain** before transformation. This is Fire (-2) at maximum development — voltage at its height, emotional pressure fully built.

The threshold between Line 3 and Line 4 is where zero-crossing occurs. Line 3 is the moment BEFORE that crossing — maximum pressure, the darkest point before dawn. After Line 4, the energy has entered matter; the descent into Earth (+4) has begun.

### Layer 3: The Channel

**Channel 36-35: Transitoriness**

Gate 36 connects to Gate 35 (Progress) through the Channel of Transitoriness.

| Gate | Type | Position |
|------|------|----------|
| 36 | Cross-Zero Manifesting | -2 → +4 |
| 35 | Cross-Zero Dematerialising | +4 → -2 |

Both gates are cross-zero, but in OPPOSITE directions!

- Gate 36: Voltage → Sink (void to maximum matter)
- Gate 35: Sink → Voltage (maximum matter to void)

**Pairing Type: Double Transformer (Opposite Directions)**

This creates an oscillator — the channel pulses between the two directions of transformation. Energy descends from feeling into density (36), then rises from density back to feeling (35). This is why it's called "Transitoriness" — nothing stays, everything is always transforming one way or the other.

### Layer 4: The Circuit

**Sensing Circuit (Collective)**

The Sensing Circuit has the highest transformation percentage of any circuit. It's dominated by the emotional wave — continuous movement, nothing static.

Gate 36-35 exemplifies this: the oscillating channel that never rests.

### Layer 5: The Centre

**Solar Plexus**

| Aspect | Value |
|--------|-------|

| Function | Emotional wave, pressure, feeling |

The Solar Plexus is voltage-dominant. It generates pressure waves — the emotional experience that fluctuates over time. Gate 36 begins here, in the pressure of emotional crisis.

### The Synthesis

**Gate 36, Line 3:**

- Cross-zero manifesting (voltage → sink, -2 → +4)
- Line 3: Peak of origin domain, maximum pressure before crossing

But the electromagnetic frame reveals: this isn't just suffering. This is the NECESSARY peak before transformation can occur. The pressure must reach maximum before it can descend into material form.

## The Method Summarised

For any line, work through the layers:

### 1. Gate Type
- Standing wave, cross-zero, or same-side?
- Which positions (origin → destination)?
- What kind of movement does this represent?

### 2. Line Position
- Which trigram (inner 1-3, outer 4-6)?
- Which stage (entry, development, completion)?
- Relationship to threshold (before, at, after crossing)?

### 3. Channel Dynamics
- What gate does this connect to?
- What pairing type (anchor+circulator, double transformer, etc.)?
- How do the two gates interact?

### 4. Circuit Architecture
- Which circuit?
- What's the transformation percentage?
- How many anchors? What kind?
- Individual, collective, or tribal?

### 5. Centre Signature
- Which centre?
- What's its electromagnetic signature?
- How does position determine function?

### Synthesis
- How do all layers combine?
- What interpretation emerges?
- How does it align with traditional meaning?
