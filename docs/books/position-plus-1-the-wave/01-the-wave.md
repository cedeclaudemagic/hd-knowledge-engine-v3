## Everything Oscillates

Watch your breath for a moment.

In. Out. In. Out.

There's a point at the top of the inhale where you're neither breathing in nor breathing out. A moment of stillness. Full.

And there's a point at the bottom of the exhale where you're neither breathing out nor breathing in. Another moment of stillness. Empty.

Between these two still points, the breath moves. Always moving. Never staying at either extreme for more than an instant before reversing.

This is oscillation. The simplest pattern in the universe.

Your heart does it — contracting, relaxing, contracting, relaxing. Day and night do it. The tides do it. Sound waves do it. Light does it. Electricity does it. The seasons do it. Pendulums do it. Springs do it.

Everything that moves, moves in waves.

---

## The Still Point

Here's what's easy to miss: the wave isn't defined by its peaks and troughs. It's defined by what's *between* them.

Think of a pendulum swinging. Left, right, left, right.

Where is the pendulum's home? Not at the far left, where it pauses for an instant before falling back. Not at the far right, where gravity pulls it down again. These are the extremes — positions of maximum displacement, maximum potential energy, maximum tension.

The pendulum's home is the centre. The lowest point. Where it moves fastest, where it would rest if nothing disturbed it, where it passes through twice on every swing but never stays.

This is the still point. Not still because nothing happens there — more happens there than anywhere else. Still because it's the reference. The zero. The place from which all displacement is measured.

Every wave has one.

---

## Two Sides of Zero

Once you have a still point, you have two sides.

The pendulum swings left of centre, then right of centre. Your breath moves above the exhale point, then below the inhale point. The tide rises above mean sea level, then falls below it.

These aren't just directions. They're *domains*. Different territories with different qualities.

Think about temperature. Zero degrees (Celsius) isn't just a number — it's where water changes state. Above zero: liquid, flowing, taking the shape of its container. Below zero: solid, crystalline, holding its own form.

The number might be arbitrary, but the threshold is real. Something different happens on each side.

Now here's the key insight: **the two sides of any wave aren't just opposites. They're complements.**

Left needs right to be left. Hot needs cold to be hot. Inhale needs exhale to be inhale. Each defines the other. Neither can exist alone.

This might seem obvious. It's actually profound. It means that every wave is secretly *one thing* expressing itself in two ways. The pendulum doesn't become a different pendulum when it swings left versus right. It's the same pendulum, the same energy, the same system — just in different phases of its cycle.

## Positions on the Wave

So we have a wave. A still point at zero. Two domains on either side.

But there's more structure here.

Imagine you're standing somewhere on a wave — not at the still point, not at the extreme, but somewhere in between. Where exactly are you?

It turns out there are meaningful positions. Not infinite gradations, but specific places where something distinct happens.

Consider the journey from zero to the peak:

First, you're barely displaced. Just past the still point. Still close to centre. The energy is just beginning to express in this direction.

Then you're further out. Momentum is building. You're clearly in this domain now, not the other.

Then you're approaching the extreme. Maximum displacement. The point where the wave must turn back.

Each of these positions has a different quality. Different potential. Different relationship to the centre and to the extreme.

The ancient Chinese noticed this. They built an entire system around it.

## Eight Places

The I Ching — the Book of Changes — is built on a structure of eight fundamental states. They called them trigrams: three-line figures, each line either solid (yang) or broken (yin).

For three thousand years, people have treated these as mystical symbols. Philosophy. Poetry. Divination.

But here's what nobody told you: **they're positions on a wave**.

Eight positions. Four on each side of zero. Arranged by their distance from the still point:

The still point itself isn't a position — it's the reference. The zero from which everything is measured.

Closest to zero, on one side: the first stirring of movement in that direction. Potential just beginning to express.

Further out: momentum building, clearly committed to this domain.

Further still: approaching the extreme, energy nearing its peak in this direction.

At the extreme: maximum displacement, the turning point where this direction has fully expressed and must begin returning.

And mirrored on the other side: four more positions, the same distances from zero, but in the complementary domain.

Eight positions. Two sides. One wave.

## Why This Matters

We're going to spend this series unpacking what those eight positions are, how they combine, and what happens when energy moves between them.

But before we go further, I want you to feel the simplicity of what we've established:

**There is a wave.**

Not a metaphor. Not a symbol. The actual pattern that underlies all change, all movement, all oscillation in the universe.

**There is a still point.**

The zero. The reference. The centre from which all positions are measured. Not empty — generative. The source of the wave itself.

**There are two domains.**

Complementary, not opposite. Each needing the other to exist. Each expressing something the other cannot.

**There are positions.**

Not infinite gradations but meaningful places. Eight of them. Four on each side. Arranged by their relationship to the centre and to the extremes.

That's it. That's the foundation.

From this simple structure — wave, zero, two sides, eight positions — we're going to derive everything. The 64 hexagrams. The 384 lines. The circuits and centres of Human Design. The electromagnetic architecture of consciousness itself.

Not because we're imposing a framework. Because the framework is already there, has always been there, waiting to be seen.

The ancients encoded it. The I Ching holds it. Human Design operationalises it.

And now we're going to understand it.
