# The Nine Centres

*Ninth in a series exploring the electromagnetic structure of consciousness*

---

## Where We Are

We've traced the journey from wave to position, from position to hexagram, from hexagram to channel, from channel to circuit. We've seen how electromagnetic architecture determines function at every level.

Now we come to the final clustering: the **centres**.

In Human Design, the bodygraph contains nine centres — energy hubs that gates connect to and through. Each centre has a set of gates. Each centre has a function.

And here's what we're about to discover: **centres cluster gates by wave position**.

This isn't random. When you look at which gates belong to each centre, patterns emerge. Some centres hold only gates at the threshold. Others hold only gates in the material domain. One centre holds both poles.

The electromagnetic profile of each centre matches its function.

---

## The Discovery

When we mapped all 64 gates to their wave positions and then looked at how they cluster into centres, something remarkable appeared.

The clustering isn't scattered. Gates don't distribute randomly across centres. Instead, each centre has a **position signature** — a characteristic clustering of wave positions that defines its electromagnetic nature.

Some signatures are absolute: every gate in the centre shares the same position.

Some signatures are dominant: most gates cluster at certain positions with a few exceptions.

Some signatures span the full range: the centre holds positions across the entire wave.

Let's meet each centre through its electromagnetic profile.

## The Spleen Centre

**Function:** Intuition, immune system, survival awareness, fear processing

**Gates:** 18, 28, 32, 44, 48, 50, 57

**Position signature: ALL gates at -1**

Every single gate in the Spleen Centre sits at position -1 — the gate out. The threshold where form dissolves toward potential. The discrimination point.

This is absolute clustering. No exceptions. Seven gates, one position.

What does this mean?

Position -1 is the **gate of discrimination**. It's where energy decides what passes and what doesn't. What crosses into potential and what stays in form. What's released and what's held.

The Spleen is pure discrimination. Its function — intuitive awareness, immune response, survival instinct — is precisely this: knowing in an instant what serves life and what threatens it. What to let through, what to reject.

The traditional description says the Spleen processes fear, operates in the moment, maintains physical wellbeing. The electromagnetic profile explains *how*: by holding the threshold position, the Spleen is literally the discriminator. Every gate asks the same question from the same position: does this pass or not?

**Electromagnetic signature:** Pure gate-threshold awareness. Instantaneous discrimination. The body's "no" when something threatens.

## The Throat Centre

**Function:** Expression, manifestation, communication, action

**Gates:** 8, 12, 16, 20, 23, 31, 33, 35, 45, 56, 62

**Position signature: ALL gates in material domain (+1 to +4)**

Every gate in the Throat Centre sits on the positive side of zero — the form domain. Positions +1, +2, +3, +4. Matter. Manifestation. The crystallised.

No Throat gate sits in potential. Everything here is already crossing into or circulating within form.

What does this mean?

The Throat is where things become *real*. Where potential becomes actual. Where the unmanifest manifests. Every gate here is either entering form (+1), flowing in form (+2), storing in form (+3), or fully crystallised (+4).

Expression requires form. Words are physical — vibrations in air. Actions are physical — bodies moving matter. The Throat takes whatever wants to express and brings it into material reality.

The traditional description says the Throat is about communication, manifestation, metamorphosis. The electromagnetic profile shows *why*: it's the centre that operates entirely in the manifest domain. Nothing here is potential. Everything here is form becoming more form, or form expressing itself as form.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Pure material expression. The crossing into actuality. Where the inner becomes outer.

## The G Centre

**Function:** Identity, direction, love, the magnetic monopole

**Gates:** 1, 2, 7, 10, 13, 15, 25, 46

**Position signature: BOTH POLES plus full distribution**

The G Centre contains both standing waves at the poles:
- Gate 1 (Heaven/Heaven) at position **-4** — the source pole
- Gate 2 (Earth/Earth) at position **+4** — the sink pole

No other centre holds both poles. The G Centre spans the complete wave.

And between the poles, its other gates distribute across multiple positions — giving it the widest range of any centre.

What does this mean?

The G Centre is the **reference frame**. It holds the full span from source to sink, from maximum potential to maximum form. It's the centre that knows where everything else is because it holds the extremes that define position itself.

This is where the magnetic monopole sits — the still point that holds Design and Personality in relationship. The centre that provides identity and direction.

The traditional description says the G Centre is about the self, about love, about where we're going. The electromagnetic profile shows *how*: by holding both poles, it's the centre that provides the reference against which everything else is measured. You can only know where you are if you know where the extremes are. The G Centre knows.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Complete reference frame. Both poles held. The centre that orients everything else.

## The Solar Plexus Centre

**Function:** Emotions, feelings, the emotional wave, desire, passion

**Gates:** 6, 22, 30, 36, 37, 49, 55

**Position signature: Dominant clustering at -2 (Voltage)**

The Solar Plexus gates cluster heavily at position -2 — the voltage position. The pressure in potential. The push that drives.

This includes Gate 30 (Fire/Fire), a standing wave at -2. Pure voltage. Sustained pressure.

What does this mean?

Voltage is *pressure*. The gradient that makes current flow. The potential difference that demands release.

The emotional wave IS pressure. Building, intensifying, seeking discharge. Hope rising, expectation building, pressure accumulating — then the crash, the release, the return to baseline. This is voltage dynamics.

The traditional description says the Solar Plexus is about emotional depth, the wave of feelings, desire and passion. The electromagnetic profile shows *why*: this centre is dominated by the pressure position. It builds charge. It creates intensity. The wave isn't arbitrary — it's voltage accumulating and discharging.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Pressure centre. Voltage dominant. The wave that builds and releases.

## The Sacral Centre

**Function:** Life force, sexuality, work capacity, response, fertility

**Gates:** 3, 5, 9, 14, 27, 29, 34, 42, 59

**Position signature: Distributed with current (+2) emphasis**

The Sacral gates distribute across multiple positions but with notable emphasis on +2 — the current position. Flow in form. Energy actually moving.

This includes Gate 29 (Water/Water), a standing wave at +2. Pure current. Sustained flow.

What does this mean?

Current is energy in motion. Not pressure (voltage) but actual flow. The life force of the Sacral isn't potential — it's kinetic. It's energy already moving, already doing, already generating.

The Sacral "response" is current switching on or off. The "uh-huh" or "uhn-uhn" is flow opening or closing. This isn't emotional pressure building — it's generative energy available or not available, right now.

The traditional description says the Sacral is life force, work energy, sexual energy, response. The electromagnetic profile shows *how*: centred on current rather than voltage, the Sacral is about energy that's already moving. Not building toward release but generating in the moment.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Generator centre. Current emphasis. Life force in motion.

## The Root Centre

**Function:** Pressure, stress, adrenaline, drive, momentum

**Gates:** 19, 38, 39, 41, 52, 53, 54, 58, 60

**Position signature: Storage positions (±3) dominant**

The Root gates cluster notably at the storage positions — both +3 (inductance/form storage) and -3 (capacitance/potential storage).

This includes two standing waves:
- Gate 52 (Mountain/Mountain) at position **+3** — form storage
- Gate 58 (Lake/Lake) at position **-3** — potential storage

What does this mean?

Storage positions hold energy. They accumulate. They create reserves — and reserves create *pressure*.

A full capacitor wants to discharge. A loaded spring wants to release. The Root's pressure isn't the emotional voltage of the Solar Plexus — it's the structural pressure of accumulated energy demanding movement.

The traditional description says the Root is about adrenalised pressure, stress, drive, the fuel to move. The electromagnetic profile shows *how*: by holding storage positions, the Root is literally where energy accumulates. Pressure here isn't emotional intensity but stored potential — the spring-loading that fuels action.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Pressure through storage. Accumulated energy. The spring before it releases.

## The Heart/Ego Centre

**Function:** Willpower, ego, self-worth, the material world, courage

**Gates:** 21, 26, 40, 51

**Position signature: Gate positions (±1) with Thunder anchor**

The Heart Centre is small — only four gates. But its signature is distinct: emphasis on the gate-threshold positions (±1), including Gate 51 (Thunder/Thunder), a standing wave at +1.

Position +1 is the gate *into* form. Where potential becomes manifest. The shock of arrival.

What does this mean?

The Heart operates at the initiation threshold. Where will becomes action. Where intention crosses into reality. Gate 51 — the Arousing, shock, the competitive spirit — sits exactly here, anchoring this function.

The traditional description says the Heart is about willpower, ego, proving oneself, material value. The electromagnetic profile shows *how*: centred on the gate into form, this is where personal will *manifests*. The crossing from intention to action. The shock that initiates.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Initiation centre. Will crossing into form. The shock of becoming material.

**Function:** Inspiration, mental pressure, questions, doubt

What does this mean?

The Head doesn't anchor or store. It transforms. Continuously. It takes cosmic input and converts it to mental pressure — the questions that demand answers.

The traditional description says the Head is about inspiration, doubt, confusion, mental pressure. The electromagnetic profile shows *how*: with no standing waves and all cross-zero gates, this centre is constant transformation. Input becoming pressure. The never-settled mind.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Transformation centre. Continuous crossing. Mental pressure through constant processing.

## The Ajna Centre

**Function:** Conceptualisation, analysis, thinking, understanding

**Gates:** 4, 11, 17, 24, 43, 47

**Position signature: Mixed with void (-) emphasis**

The Ajna gates cluster toward the potential (void) side of the wave. They process in the realm of concept, pattern, possibility — before materialisation.

What does this mean?

The Ajna thinks before speaking. It processes in potential before expressing in form. Concepts are refined, patterns are recognised, understanding develops — all in the mental space before manifestation.

The traditional description says the Ajna is about how we think, conceptualise, process. The electromagnetic profile shows *where*: primarily in the void domain. The Ajna is upstream of the Throat. It works with potential; the Throat makes it manifest.

**Electromagnetic signature:** Conceptual processing. Void emphasis. Patterns before expression.
