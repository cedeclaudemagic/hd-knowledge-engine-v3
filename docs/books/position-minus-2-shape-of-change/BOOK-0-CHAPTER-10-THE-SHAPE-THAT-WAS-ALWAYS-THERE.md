## What This Changes

Knowing the geometry changes how you work with the I Ching.

**Before:** Hexagram meanings are memorised or intuitively received. Why Hexagram 11 means "Peace" and Hexagram 12 means "Standstill" is accepted on authority.

**After:** You can *see* why. Heaven below rising toward Earth above descending — convergent flow — peace. Earth below sinking away from Heaven above rising — divergent flow — standstill. The meaning is readable from the geometry.

**Before:** The system seems arbitrary. Why 64 hexagrams? Why 8 trigrams? Why 6 lines? Tradition says so.

**After:** The numbers are necessary. 8 = 2³ = cube vertices. 64 = 8² = movements through the cube. 6 = octahedron vertices = cube face directions. The structure couldn't be otherwise.

**Before:** Working with the I Ching feels like consulting an oracle — mysterious, unpredictable, dependent on faith.

**After:** Working with the I Ching feels like reading a map — structured, navigable, dependent on understanding. Mystery remains, but it's mystery within a known architecture.

Both cubes trace spiral paths through their corners. The paths interweave, chasing each other, never quite touching, never fully separating.

At the centre of both cubes — the axis, the diagonal from Earth to Heaven — is nothing. Empty space. The void that makes rotation possible.
