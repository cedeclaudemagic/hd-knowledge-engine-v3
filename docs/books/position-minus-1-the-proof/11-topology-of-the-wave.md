A figure-8 is the 2D projection of a **(2,1) torus knot** — a path that wraps:
- **2 times** around the tube of a torus (minor radius)
- **1 time** around the hole of a torus (major radius)

```

      - q times around the ring (complete circuits)
    
    The wheel makes:
      - 2 domain crossings per cycle
```
