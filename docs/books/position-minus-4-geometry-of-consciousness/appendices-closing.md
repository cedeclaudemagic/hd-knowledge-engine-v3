## 216: The Cube of Six

The number 216 appears as the "pre-Base" structure: 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 positions per line before the Base differentiation.

In Hebrew gematria:
- **גבורה (Gevurah)** = 216 — "Strength" or "Severity," one of the ten Sefirot in Kabbalah

Gevurah is the sphere of judgment, limitation, and form. It is the constraining principle that gives structure to existence. The I Ching's 216 is literally a "cube" (6³), and the cube is the form-giving solid.

Additionally:
- 216 = 6 × 6 × 6 (the "number of the beast" in some interpretations)
- 216 = sum of cubes of 3, 4, 5 (the Pythagorean triple): 27 + 64 + 125 = 216

## 72 and the Names of God

The number 72 appears in Kabbalistic tradition as the number of names of God, derived from three verses of Exodus (14:19-21), each containing 72 letters.

- 72 × 3 = 216 (the total letters in the 72 names)
- 72 = 8 × 9 = trigrams × lines-per-trigram-pair

The 72 names are associated with transformative power — the ability to invoke divine aspects for specific purposes. The I Ching's transformative system (nuclear hexagrams, changing lines) may encode a parallel structure.

## 64 and Din

In Hebrew:
- **דין (Din)** = 64 — "Judgment" or "Law"

Din is related to Gevurah (216) — both concern structure, limitation, and the lawful ordering of existence. The 64 hexagrams are the "laws" of change, the complete set of transformational patterns.

## 180 and the Half-Turn

180° is π radians — the half-turn, the measure of opposition.

Each line spans 180 sub-positions (6 × 6 × 5 = 180). This is the line as "half a rotation" — from one pole of an axis to the other.

The number 180 appears in many sacred contexts:
- 180° = angle of opposition in astrology
- 180° = the "inconjunct" angle of tension
- 180 = 4 × 45 = four quarter-turns of the octave

## 384 and the Lines

The I Ching has 384 lines (64 × 6).

- 384 = 2⁷ × 3 = 128 × 3 = 256 × 1.5

The number 384 is close to 360 + 24 = the circle plus the hours of a day. It is also close to 365 + 19 ≈ 384, suggesting a lunisolar cycle relationship.

In music, 384 = 256 × 1.5 — which is C × a perfect fifth. The musical connection deserves investigation.

---

## The 3D Model

On a flat wheel, all four points are coplanar. Four coplanar points cannot form a tetrahedron.

However, Design and Personality operate on different "layers" — separated by 88 days in time. We model this as a z-offset:

- Personality layer: z = 0
- Design layer: z = h (height offset)

For our calculation, we use h = 1 (equal to the wheel radius).

**Cartesian Coordinates:**

| Node | x | y | z |
|------|---|---|---|
| P-North | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0 |
| P-South | -1.000 | 0.000 | 0 |
| D-North | 0.035 | -0.999 | 1 |
| D-South | -0.035 | 0.999 | 1 |

---

## The Six Edges

A tetrahedron has 6 edges. We calculate all distances:

```python
def distance(p1, p2):
    return sqrt(sum((a-b)² for a,b in zip(p1,p2)))
```

| Edge | Vertices | Length |
|------|----------|--------|
| P-N ↔ P-S | Personality diagonal | 2.000 |
| D-N ↔ D-S | Design diagonal | 2.000 |
| P-N ↔ D-N | Same-direction N | 1.712 |
| P-S ↔ D-S | Same-direction S | 1.712 |
| P-N ↔ D-S | Cross N→S | 1.752 |
| P-S ↔ D-N | Cross S→N | 1.752 |

---

## Disphenoid Confirmation

A disphenoid is a tetrahedron where opposite edges are equal. We check:

- Diagonals: 2.000 = 2.000 ✓
- Same-direction: 1.712 = 1.712 ✓
- Cross: 1.752 = 1.752 ✓

**The four Nodes form a disphenoid.**

---

## The Three Edge Lengths

| Edge Type | Length | Interpretation |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Diagonals | 2.000 | The two 180° axes (N-S pairs) |
| Same-direction | 1.712 | North-to-North, South-to-South |
| Cross | 1.752 | Opposite nodes connected |

The ratio of Cross to Same-direction:
```
1.752 / 1.712 = 1.0235 (2.35% difference)
```

This 2.35% asymmetry encodes the 2° angular offset (90° - 88° = 2°).

---

## Volume Calculation

The volume of a tetrahedron with vertices A, B, C, D is:

```
Volume = |det([B-A, C-A, D-A])| / 6
```

For our four Nodes:
```
Volume = 0.6663 (at h = 1)
```

Compare to a perfect tetrahedron at 90° offset:
```
Volume at 90° = 0.6667 = 2/3 exactly
```

The 88° offset reduces the volume to **99.94% of 2/3**.
