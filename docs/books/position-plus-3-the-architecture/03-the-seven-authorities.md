# The Seven Authorities

*Third chapter of Position +3: The Architecture*

## The Noise and the Signal

You're trying to make a decision.

Something is being offered — a job, a relationship, a move, an opportunity. Part of you says yes. Part of you says no. Part of you says maybe, wait, what do other people think, what's the smart thing to do, what would my mother say?

The mind spins. It generates reasons. It can argue both sides with equal conviction. And somewhere underneath all that noise, there's a signal — something that actually *knows*.

The question is: how do you hear it?

Human Design calls this your Authority. It's the part of your design that's reliable for decision-making — the inner signal you can actually trust. And there are exactly seven of them, arranged in a precise hierarchy.

Emotional Authority trumps Sacral. Sacral trumps Splenic. Splenic trumps Ego. And so on.

Why? Why does the Solar Plexus get to override the Sacral response? Why does the Spleen's flash of intuition outrank the Heart's willpower?

The answer isn't tradition. It's physics.

The seven Authorities are the seven ways a human system can have a reliable inner signal — and their hierarchy is determined by which signals depend on which other signals for accurate reading.

It's not preference. It's signal processing.

Each of the nine centres occupies a position on the wave. These positions aren't arbitrary labels — they describe actual electromagnetic functions:

| Position | Name | Function |
|---|---|---|
| -4 | Source | Origin, pure potential |
| -3 | Capacitance | Stored energy, pressure |

The centres cluster at specific positions:

- **Solar Plexus** sits at **-2** (Voltage)
- **Sacral** sits at **+2** (Current)

- **Heart** sits at **+1** (Threshold/Initiation)
- **G Centre** sits at **±4** (Poles — both Source and Sink)

These aren't metaphors. They describe what each centre *does* electromagnetically.

## Why Emotional Is First

Here's a piece of basic physics.

Power equals voltage times current.

*P = V × I*

If voltage fluctuates — if it rises and falls in a wave pattern — then *power* fluctuates too. Even if current stays constant, power oscillates with the voltage.

Now translate this into the body.

The Solar Plexus sits at the voltage position. When it's defined, it creates an emotional wave — a fluctuation in amplitude that affects *everything else in the system*.

At the peak of the wave, all signals are amplified. Everything looks good. You're excited, hopeful, certain. The Sacral says YES. The Spleen says GO. The Heart says ABSOLUTELY.

At the trough of the wave, all signals are diminished. Everything looks problematic. You're doubtful, flat, uncertain. The same Sacral that said yes now feels muted. The Spleen's knowing is buried under heaviness. The Heart can't find its will.

Neither reading is accurate.

The only accurate reading comes at the *baseline* — when the wave has gone through enough of its cycle that you're no longer at a peak or a trough, but somewhere that reflects the true signal.

This is why Emotional Authority comes first. Not because the Solar Plexus is "more important" than other centres. Because when voltage is fluctuating, *you cannot accurately read any other signal*.

It's physics. The wave modulates everything.

If you have a defined Solar Plexus, your Authority is Emotional. Full stop. No other centre can override it, because every other centre's signal is being modulated by your emotional wave.

The strategy — "wait for clarity, there is no truth in the now" — isn't a spiritual teaching. It's a description of wave mechanics. You have to wait for enough of the cycle to pass that you're no longer reading an amplified or diminished signal.

## The Signal Dependency Chain

Once we understand that voltage modulates current, the rest of the hierarchy falls into place.

Each electromagnetic position *depends* on positions above it in the chain. You can't get an accurate reading at one level until the levels above have stabilised.

**Amplitude → Flow → Threshold → Initiation → Reference**

Or in Authority terms:

**Emotional → Sacral → Splenic → Ego → Self-Projected**

Let me unpack this.

---

### Sacral Authority (Position +2)

The Sacral sits at the current position — sustained flow.

When there's no emotional wave (Solar Plexus undefined), the Sacral signal is clean. It's binary: on or off, yes or no, *uh-huh* or *uhn-uhn*. The gut response that happens instantly, before the mind can intervene.

Sacral Authority is *instantaneous*. The signal doesn't need time to process. It doesn't wave. It responds — right now — to what's in front of you.

The experience of Sacral Authority is that immediate body knowing. Something shows up, and your gut answers. The sound, the feeling, the energetic yes or no. If you override it with your mind, you feel the wrongness — the frustration of going against your flow.

Sacral outranks everything below it because at the current position, the signal is about *sustained flow*. If the flow is wrong, nothing downstream will work right. The life force itself is saying no.

---

### Splenic Authority (Position -1)

The Spleen sits at the gate-out position — the threshold of discrimination.

When there's no emotional wave AND no Sacral definition, the Splenic signal becomes primary. This is the quiet knowing, the body's ancient awareness of what's safe and what isn't.

Splenic Authority speaks *once*. It's a flash — instant recognition that something is right or wrong for your survival, your health, your wellbeing. Unlike the Sacral's ongoing availability, the Spleen speaks once and then goes quiet. If you miss it, it doesn't repeat.

The position explains this. At the threshold (gate-out), something either crosses or it doesn't. There's no sustained signal, no wave, no ongoing flow. Just the moment of recognition: safe or not safe, healthy or unhealthy, yes or no.

Splenic Authority is the fastest — faster even than Sacral, in a sense. But it's also the quietest. The body whispers. If the mind is loud, you miss it.

---

### Ego Authority (Position +1)

The Heart sits at the gate-in position — the threshold of initiation.

When there's no Emotional, Sacral, or Splenic Authority, the Heart's signal becomes primary. This is willpower — the capacity to commit, to promise, to follow through.

Ego Authority operates through commitment. "What's in it for me?" isn't a selfish question — it's the Heart's way of testing whether this is something it can actually will itself toward. If the Heart can commit, it's correct. If it can't, it isn't.

Unlike Splenic Authority's one-time flash, Ego Authority is *renewable*. The will can reassert. You can check in again tomorrow and find the commitment still there — or discover it's gone.

The position explains this. At the initiation threshold (gate-in), what matters is whether energy is entering, whether will is engaging. That engagement can be tested and retested. The will shows up or it doesn't.

---

### Self-Projected Authority (Position ±4)

The G Centre sits at the polar positions — both source and sink, the complete reference frame.

When there's no Emotional, Sacral, Splenic, or Ego Authority, the G Centre's signal becomes primary. This is identity — direction, love, the sense of self.

Self-Projected Authority works through *resonance*. You need to speak to hear your truth. The signal doesn't exist cleanly inside — it needs to be externalised, verbalised, sounded out. When you hear yourself say something true, you recognise it. When you hear yourself say something false, you notice the dissonance.

The position explains this. At the poles, you're at the reference frame itself — the place from which all other positions are measured. But a reference frame only works when it's *in relationship* to something. You can't know yourself in isolation at ±4. You need the reflection, the sounding board, the act of expression that shows you where you stand.

---

### Mental Authority (No defined authority centres)

When none of the five authority centres are defined — no Solar Plexus, Sacral, Spleen, Heart, or G Centre — there's no reliable inner signal to read.

What remains? Head, Ajna, Throat. Processing and expression. The mind.

Mental Authority doesn't mean you decide with your mind. It means you have no *inner* authority — no electromagnetic position that reliably signals. Instead, you need *outer* authority. Sounding boards. Trusted others who can reflect your process back to you.

The experience of Mental Authority is needing to talk things through. Not to get advice, but to hear yourself. The truth emerges in dialogue, not in isolation.

---

### Lunar Authority (No defined centres)

The Reflector — the one with no centres defined at all.

No fixed electromagnetic position anywhere. Every centre is open, conditioned, reflecting the environment and the transits.

Lunar Authority is the ultimate in waiting. The Moon moves through every gate over its 28-day cycle. A Reflector needs to sample *all* of those positions before making a major decision. Not because they're slow, but because they have no fixed signal. They need to experience themselves in every configuration before they know what's consistent.

The strategy — wait a lunar cycle — is geometric. It takes 28 days for the Moon to transit all 64 gates, creating temporary definition in each centre. Over that cycle, the Reflector experiences themselves in every possible configuration. What remains consistent across all of those experiences is what's true.

## The Hierarchy Is Physics

Let's put this together.

| Rank | Authority | Position | Why This Order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emotional | -2 | Voltage modulates all other signals |
| 2 | Sacral | +2 | Current (flow) requires stable voltage |
| 3 | Splenic | -1 | Threshold requires stable flow |
| 4 | Ego | +1 | Initiation requires clear discrimination |
| 5 | Self-Projected | ±4 | Reference requires all signals clear |
| 6 | Mental | — | No internal signal; needs external |
| 7 | Lunar | — | No fixed position; samples all |

Each level depends on the levels above. You can't read flow accurately if amplitude is fluctuating. You can't discriminate clearly if flow is unstable. You can't initiate truly if discrimination is clouded.

The hierarchy isn't arbitrary ranking. It's *signal dependency*. Each electromagnetic position requires stability at all positions above it before it can be reliably read.

## Receive Before Express

There's a sub-pattern worth noticing.

At each magnitude, the negative position outranks the positive.

-2 (Emotional) outranks +2 (Sacral).
-1 (Splenic) outranks +1 (Ego).

Why?

In electromagnetic terms, negative is *receiving*. Positive is *expressing*.

You have to receive before you can express. You have to take in the signal before you can act on it.

The emotional wave receives the fullness of experience — all the highs and lows — before you can respond with current. The splenic threshold discriminates — receives information about safety — before the heart can initiate.

Receiving comes before expressing. The negative before the positive. Not as judgment, but as sequence.

## What Your Authority Feels Like

If you have **Emotional Authority**, you feel the wave. The ups and downs, the shifting certainty, the way everything looks different at different times. Your work is patience — not acting on the peak, not reacting to the trough, but waiting for enough of the cycle that you're reading something true. The challenge is the culture that wants immediate answers. Your gift is the depth that comes from riding the full wave.

If you have **Sacral Authority**, you feel the gut. The immediate knowing that doesn't come from thought. Something shows up and your body responds — pulled toward or pushed away. The sound, the energy, the visceral yes or no. Your work is to respond rather than initiate, to trust the gut over the mind. The challenge is everyone who asks you to explain. Your gift is the life force that builds what's correct.

If you have **Splenic Authority**, you feel the flash. The quiet knowing that speaks once and goes. Something is safe or unsafe, healthy or unhealthy, and you know it in an instant — before thought, before explanation. Your work is to catch the whisper, to act on the first recognition before the mind overwrites it. The challenge is how quiet it is. Your gift is the ancient survival intelligence that protects and guides.

If you have **Ego Authority**, you feel the will. The capacity to commit or not, to promise or not, to follow through or not. "What's in it for me?" is your question — not selfishness, but the test of true engagement. Your work is to honour what you can actually commit to. The challenge is a world that moralises against self-interest. Your gift is willpower that holds its word.

If you have **Self-Projected Authority**, you feel the resonance. Truth that emerges in speaking, identity that clarifies through expression. You need to talk — not for advice, but to hear yourself. Your work is finding the right listeners, the ones who let you sound yourself out. The challenge is knowing you can't figure it out silently. Your gift is the clarity that comes when you hear yourself speak what's true.

If you have **Mental Authority**, you feel the need for dialogue. No inner signal to rely on — you genuinely need others to reflect your process. Not to tell you what to do, but to be the sounding board that lets you hear your own truth. Your work is building those trusted relationships. The challenge is a culture that prizes independence. Your gift is wisdom that emerges through connection.

If you have **Lunar Authority**, you feel the shifting. Everything moving, everything changing, no fixed point. Your work is patience beyond what anyone else requires — waiting for the Moon to move through its entire cycle before you know what's consistent. The challenge is a world that wants fast answers. Your gift is showing the community itself — reflecting what is, without distortion.

## Seven and Only Seven

Every possible configuration of nine centres — all 512 patterns — fits exactly one of these seven Authority categories.

Emotional: 50%
Sacral: 25%
Splenic: 12.5%
Ego: 6.3%
Self-Projected: 3.1%
Mental: 2.9%
Lunar: 0.2%

The percentages are emergent. Each Authority requires certain centres to be defined and others undefined. The gate structure — how many gates each centre has, how likely they are to form channels — determines how common each Authority is.

Emotional Authority is most common because the Solar Plexus has many gates and sits at the top of the hierarchy. Lunar Authority is rarest because it requires zero definition — every possible activation must miss.

But whatever your configuration, you have exactly one Authority. The hierarchy ensures this: whichever defined centre sits highest in the chain is your Authority. No gaps, no overlaps, no ambiguity.

## The Physics of Trust

Your Authority isn't something you develop. It's something you *have*.

The question isn't how to get better at it. The question is whether you're listening to it.

The emotional person learns to wait, even when the culture demands immediate answers. The sacral person learns to trust the grunt, even when it can't be explained. The splenic person learns to catch the whisper before the mind drowns it out.

Each Authority is a signal at a specific electromagnetic position. The hierarchy ensures you know which signal to prioritise. The strategy follows from the position — wave mechanics for Emotional, instant response for Sacral, flash recognition for Splenic, willpower test for Ego, verbal resonance for Self-Projected, dialogue for Mental, lunar sampling for Reflector.

It's not mysticism. It's physics.

And the physics says: your signal is reliable. You just have to learn to hear it clearly, and to stop letting the mind convince you it knows better.

The mind is a wonderful tool. It just isn't your Authority.
