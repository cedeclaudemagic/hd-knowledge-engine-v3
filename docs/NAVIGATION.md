# HD Knowledge Engine V3: Navigation Guide

## The Core Distinction

This project embodies a fundamental distinction:

| Category | Meaning | Location |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **PROVEN** | Geometrically derived, mathematically necessary | `/framework/` |
| **OUTSTANDING** | Not yet derived, pressure remains | `/investigations/` |

**If it's not proven, it's under investigation. There is no middle ground.**

---

## Quick Start

### Finding Proven Derivations
Go to `/framework/`. Everything there is settled.

### Finding Open Questions
Go to `/investigations/active/`. The work continues there.

### Understanding Current Limits
Go to `/investigations/boundaries/`. These are pressure points, not permanent limits.

### Reading the Books
Go to `/books/`. Narrative explanations of proven structure.
